Imaginary planet No. 031b: Ruby. A planet for embarrassing dads.

On Ruby, the sky blushes for you: whenever anyone on the planet is embarrassed it goes a little pink, and red when it's bad. Last week the evening newsreader signed off with 'Love you, bye,' and the sky stayed red for a week. On day eight it came back blue, and so did he. He ended the news with a firm handshake, to camera. He didn't blush; he's done twenty years of news. The sky went pink anyway. His teenagers were watching.
