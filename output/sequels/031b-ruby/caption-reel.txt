Invented planet No. 031b: Ruby. A planet for embarrassing dads.

Those two outside the house are his.

This is part two of Ruby. Last week the evening newsreader ended the news with 'Love you, bye,' live, to eleven million people. Four million said it back, and the sky stayed red for a week.

Ruby is a small blue planet of twelve million people where the sky does your blushing for you: a little pink whenever anyone is embarrassed, red when it's bad. Everyone can see it, so nobody gets away with anything.

On day eight the sky came back blue, and so did he. He'd had a week to think. At the end of the news he looked down the lens, held out his hand and offered the nation a firm handshake. He didn't blush. He's done twenty years of news. The sky went pink anyway, from a house on the edge of town where his two teenagers had been watching.

I watched it in a café, and shook back. Some of that pink was mine.

(German has one word for being embarrassed on somebody else's behalf: Fremdscham. On Ruby, it's called having a dad.)
