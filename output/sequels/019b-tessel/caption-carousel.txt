Imaginary planet No. 019b: Tessel. A planet for the double-checker.

Winter on Tessel lasts a year, with no sun and no calendar, so every village has someone who keeps the date. Jess keeps it on a board by her door, and her wages are the first slice of every birthday cake. The winter she lost count, the village kept the board for her. When she got her chalk back, she checked their sums and found Day 213 on there twice. It was her birthday: the village had wanted more cake. Jess has never let a sum go in her life. She found no mistakes.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
