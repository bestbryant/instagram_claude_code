Imaginary planet No. 003b: Hush. Hush, part two. Her answer finally crossed the valley.

On Thursday afternoon, as promised, her answer came across the valley. Half the town stood in the snow to hear it. It was yes. Then, about four seconds later and very faintly, a second word: 'obviously'. They are getting married in spring. The vows have to be shouted in January to arrive on time.

What would you shout across the valley?
