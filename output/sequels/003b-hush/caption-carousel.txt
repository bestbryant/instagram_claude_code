Imaginary planet No. 003b: Hush. A planet for couples who bicker.

Sound is slow on Hush: a shout takes five days to cross the valley, so wedding vows are shouted five days ahead and land on the day. The band forgot. The man who proposed across the valley in March and the woman who walked over with her answer married in the first snow, in the chapel on the far side, and the band played far too loudly. Five days later, at home, they had their first row, about the band. Halfway through, the wedding arrived across the valley, faint and slow: the cheering, the speeches, and then the song from their first dance. They danced. They were still furious.
