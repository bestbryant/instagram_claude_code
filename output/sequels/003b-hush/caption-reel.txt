Invented planet No. 003b: Hush. A planet for couples who bicker.

The song in the first second is theirs.

This is part two of Hush. In March he shouted a proposal across the valley, and in October she walked over with her answer.

Hush is a long, snowed-in valley where sound crawls along slower than you can walk, and a shout takes five days to cross it. People plan around it. Wedding vows are shouted five days ahead, so they land on the day. The band forgot.

They married in the first snow, in the chapel on the far side, and the band played far too loudly. Five days later, at home, they had their first row. It was about the band. Halfway through, the wedding came drifting in across the valley: the cheering, the speeches, and then the song from their first dance.

I was a guest. I clapped at the end of their first dance, so five days later I also clapped at the end of their row.

(On Earth, sound crosses a kilometre of air in about three seconds, a little longer in the cold. On Hush, allow five days.)

Who said sorry first? Nobody knows yet. It's on its way.
