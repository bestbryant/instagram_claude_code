On Hush, walking is quicker than shouting. (Imaginary planet No. 003b: Hush)

Part one is No. 003, a few posts down: in March he shouted a proposal across the valley, and her answer was due on Thursday.

She shouted her answer and set off straight after it. On Wednesday night she found him on the valley floor, where he'd been all week, and walked him home through snow up to her knees. They sat up by the stove and said nothing. You never spoil a shout.

On Thursday the bakery shut at three. Half the town stood on the valley floor with flasks, not talking, so as not to drown it out. At ten past four the snow stopped, as if it wanted to listen too.

Custom says you answer a shout with a shout, so he did, with her standing next to him. It reaches her empty house on Tuesday. I'm walking round to hear it, and I'll put what he said in the comments.

The wedding is in spring. The vows go out five days early.

What would you shout across the valley?
