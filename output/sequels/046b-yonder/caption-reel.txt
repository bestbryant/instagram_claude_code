Invented planet No. 046b: Yonder. A planet for the grandkids.

Nan can still see that gate.

This is part two of Yonder. In part one, Nan waved you off after Sunday lunch, and when you got home four days later, she was still waving.

Yonder is 56,000 people on a planet that's flat from pole to pole, with no weather to speak of and air so clear that nothing ever goes out of sight. Waving someone off lasts exactly as long as their walk home.

One Sunday, Nan came to yours, so it was your turn at the gate. You swapped arms at teatime. She never has. On day two, you put the kettle on. She was still waving when you came back out with the tea. She's been lovely about it.

I made a cup of tea on my last morning there. A man two days' walk away raised his.

(In 2016, a photographer in the Pyrenees took a picture of a mountain in the Alps, 443 kilometres away. On Yonder, you'd still be waving.)

Would you have put the kettle on?
