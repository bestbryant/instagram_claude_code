Imaginary planet No. 046b: Yonder. A planet for the grandkids.

Yonder is flat from pole to pole, with no hills, no bends and no weather to speak of, and the air is so clear that nothing ever goes out of sight. So when Nan leaves, you wave her off at the gate until she's out of sight, the way you do. On Yonder, that takes four days. One Sunday, Nan came to yours, so it was your turn. You swapped arms at teatime; she never has. On day two, you put the kettle on. She was still waving when you came back out. She's been lovely about it.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
