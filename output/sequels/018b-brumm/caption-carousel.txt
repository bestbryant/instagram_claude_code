Imaginary planet No. 018b: Brumm. A planet for whoever carried you.

Gravity on Brumm is twice ours, so by four a child is too heavy to carry far, and in the week they get too heavy, every child gets one Last Carry, wherever they like. Tomas picked the sea, eight miles away, and slept through it. At the sea, his dad turned round. Home was eight miles away too, all of it uphill. There's a bench every half mile on that road, and his dad didn't use one. Tomas woke up in his own bed with sand in his socks. His dad's knees still mention it.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
