Invented planet No. 018b: Brumm. A planet for whoever carried you.

He's facing the wrong way for home.

This is part two of Brumm. In part one, Tomas got his Last Carry and picked the sea, eight miles from home. His dad made it. Tomas was asleep by mile six.

Brumm is 140,000 people under twice our gravity. By four, a child is too heavy to carry far, so in the week they get too heavy, every child gets one Last Carry, wherever they like. Most pick the bakery.

At the sea, his dad turned round. Home was eight miles away too, all of it uphill. There's a bench every half mile on that road. He didn't use one. Tomas woke up in his own bed with sand in his socks. His dad's knees still mention it.

I walked back with them, carrying nothing, and sat on all sixteen benches.

(On Earth, each step of a walk puts more than two and a half times your body weight through the knee. We know because a few people with knee replacements have walked about with sensors inside them. On Brumm, double it, and add Tomas.)

When did someone last carry you home asleep?
