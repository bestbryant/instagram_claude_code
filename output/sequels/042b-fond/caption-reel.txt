Leave any name. I'll find its planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Invented planet No. 042b: Fond. A planet for mummy's boys.

His only one goes in the first second.

This is part two of Fond. In part one, Sam left for the city and spent his one 'I love you' on his mum, Kath, on the station platform. She said, 'Take a coat.'

Fond is 880,000 people, long snowy winters and one 'I love you' each. Say it a second time and it comes out as a cough, so people keep theirs and say 'Have you got your keys?' instead.

In the city, Sam met Maya. He held out until February. Then, on her doorstep, he told her anyway, and it came out as a cough. Maya looked at him for a long time. Then she coughed back. She'd spent hers years ago, on a pony called Toffee.

I had a cold the week I was there. Four strangers coughed back.

(The story goes that the novelist Natsume Soseki, teaching English, told a student that nobody in Japan would say 'I love you'; they'd say 'The moon is beautiful, isn't it?' On Fond, you can say that one as often as you like.)

How long would you have held out?
