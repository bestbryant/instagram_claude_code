Imaginary planet No. 042b: Fond. A planet for mummy's boys.

On Fond, everyone gets one 'I love you'; say it a second time and it comes out as a cough. Sam spent his on his mum, Kath, at the station, the day he left for the city. In the city he met Maya, and held out until February. Then, on her doorstep, he told her anyway, and it came out as a cough. Maya looked at him for a long time. Then she coughed back. She'd spent hers years ago, on a pony.
