Imaginary planet No. 011b: Bramble. A planet for the family cook.

Trees on Bramble never stop growing. The tallest has a small rain cloud of its own, and near the top is a village that hasn't come down in six hundred years. It has its own kitchens. Their family on the ground cooks for them anyway, and every evening a pinecone drops with the same answer: 'Coming!' On Wednesday, someone came down. It took him a day. Agnes had the table laid before he touched the ground. He'd already eaten. She poured him a cup anyway.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
