Invented planet No. 011b: Bramble. A planet for the family cook.

Every light up that tree is a kitchen.

This is part two of Bramble. In part one, a village near the top of the tallest tree hadn't come down in six hundred years, and every evening the family on the ground called them for dinner and got the same pinecone back: 'Coming!'

Trees on Bramble never stop growing. It's a day's climb down and a week back up, so nobody bothers, and the village has its own rain cloud and its own kitchens. Agnes has cooked for the top of the tree for fifty-one years anyway.

On Wednesday, someone came down. It took him a day. Agnes had the table laid before he touched the ground. He'd already eaten. She poured him a cup anyway.

I was standing right there. I hadn't eaten. Nobody asked.

(On Earth, a redwood can't pull water much higher than 122 to 130 metres, so that's about as tall as a tree can get; the tallest known, Hyperion in California, is about 116. Bramble's tallest tree is watered from the top.)

Would you have fed him anyway?
