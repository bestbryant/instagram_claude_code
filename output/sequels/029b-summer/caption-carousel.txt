Imaginary planet No. 029b: Summer. A planet for competitive nans.

On Summer, it snows once every eighty years. By the end, Nan was the only one left who'd seen the last one, and she had mentioned it at every wedding and every funeral for eighty years. Then it snowed again, and it squeaked, as she'd said. Now everyone has seen snow, and everyone mentions it at every wedding and every funeral. Nan has heard enough. At the next wedding, she stood up and said she's seen a dragon.
