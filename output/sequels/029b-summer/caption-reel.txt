Invented planet No. 029b: Summer. A planet for competitive nans.

Nan's the one without a parasol.

This is part two of Summer. In part one, it snowed for the first time in eighty years, and Nan, who had been describing the last snowfall since she was six, turned out to be right. It squeaked.

Summer is 210,000 people on a world where it's always summer, apart from one snowfall every eighty years, give or take. Coats are museum pieces, and at school, snow is taught next to dragons.

By the end, Nan was the only one left who'd seen it, and for eighty years she had mentioned it at every wedding and every funeral. Then it snowed again. Now all 210,000 people have seen snow, and they mention it at every wedding and every funeral. Nan has heard enough. At the next wedding, she stood up during the speeches and said she's seen a dragon.

I was at that wedding. I'd just got to the part where it squeaks.

(Earth has dragons. In 1910, rumours of a 'land crocodile' reached a Dutch officer in what is now Indonesia, and he went and got one: the Komodo dragon, up to three metres long. Nobody on Summer has gone to look.)

Do you believe her this time?
