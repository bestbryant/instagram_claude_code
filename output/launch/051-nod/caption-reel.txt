A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 051: Nod. A planet for every dad who's just resting his eyes.

Every kite on that hill is tied to one nap.

Nod: 12,000 people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and no weather of its own. The wind is Dad, snoring in an armchair on the hill: light breathing for a breeze, a proper snore for a gale. Kite season is the hour after the roast. Babies are rocked to sleep by it. Nobody has told him.

I sneezed during a gust. Three kites and a hat are still in the next valley.

(The story goes that 'the land of Nod' only came to mean sleep in 1738, when Jonathan Swift made a pun of it. Before that, it was east of Eden. Here, it's east of the armchair.)

How many kites would your house fly after lunch?
