Imaginary planet No. 051: Nod. A planet for every dad who's just resting his eyes.

On Nod, the wind is Dad, snoring. All week the air is still. Then, after the big lunch, he settles into his armchair on the hill, and within ten minutes the whole town is flying kites. Nobody runs the hoover. Nobody drops anything. Then you drop a spoon. Every kite comes down, and everyone looks at you.
