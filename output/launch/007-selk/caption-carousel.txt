Imaginary planet No. 007: Selk. A planet for people who go to the beach and don't swim.

Selk used to be an ocean. When the water went, the waves stayed where they were, frozen mid-break in green glass, some of them two hundred metres high. Surfers still make the trip. They carry their boards across the sand, stand under the curl for about an hour, take a photo and go home. One of them told me it was the best wave he'd ever had, and he meant it.

Would you make the trip?
