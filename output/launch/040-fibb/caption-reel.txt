Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 040: Fibb. A planet for people who've oversold themselves on a first date.

Everything in this picture is true. For now.

Fibb is 2.1 million people on a quiet, grassy world where anything you say that isn't true is true by morning. Children learn to be precise before they learn to be polite. CVs are accurate, eventually. Politicians speak very slowly.

I told a customs officer I had nothing to declare. In the morning I had nothing at all, including my map.

(The story goes that the English 'fib' began as 'fible-fable', an old word for nonsense. On Fibb, there's no such thing as a small one.)
