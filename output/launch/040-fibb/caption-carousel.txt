Imaginary planet No. 040: Fibb. A planet for people who've oversold themselves on a first date.

On Fibb, every lie comes true by morning, so fishermen are very popular. On their first date, Ned told Iris he lived by the sea. By morning, he did. Date two: a lighthouse. Date three: two moons. On date four, Iris said she'd liked date one better, so he told her the truth: it's a field, and he's just Ned. By then, that was a lie. By morning it was a field again. She stayed. She does miss the moons.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
