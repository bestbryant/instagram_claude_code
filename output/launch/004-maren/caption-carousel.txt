Imaginary planet No. 004: Maren. A planet where 'your parcel has been delivered' means look up.

At some point the mountains on Maren lifted off the ground and stayed up. Low gravity and a long time, apparently. They farm the tops now, and rope ladders hang down to the valley. The post goes up on a very long pole, held by a woman called Ines who has opinions about pole length. She has never lost a letter. She has lost a great many poles.

Would you live up top or in the valley?
