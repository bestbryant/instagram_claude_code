At some point the mountains on Maren lifted off the ground and didn't come back down. Low gravity and a long time, people say. They farm the tops now. Rope ladders go up from the valley floor, and the post is delivered by a woman with a very long pole. The oldest island has a single pine on it. Children climb it once, on their tenth birthday, and are never quite as keen on heights again.
