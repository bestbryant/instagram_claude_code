A planet where 'your parcel has been delivered' means look up. (Imaginary planet No. 004: Maren)

Gravity on Maren is about a third of yours, and long ago the mountains lifted off and stayed up, farms and all. The valley calls them upstairs. Most of Maren's 610,000 people live up there and have never been down. Rope ladders hang for weddings and emergencies, and nobody likes them, so the post goes up at first light on a twelve-metre pole. Ines thinks anything longer is cheating. At a third of a g, a dropped thing takes nearly twice as long to land as it would here, which gives you time to see what it is and decide whether you want it. On a still night in the valley, you can hear upstairs snoring.

I was in the valley for her last round, and something landed on me too: pole number 312. By law, it's mine. Ines has written to me about it twice. She spelled my name wrong both times.

What's the best thing that's ever landed on you?

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
