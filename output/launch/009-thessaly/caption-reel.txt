On Thessaly the rain goes up. It lifts off the sea in thin threads and collects in shallow pools on the undersides of the clouds, which catch the light at dusk. People wear their umbrellas on their feet. The pools in the sky all have names, and children argue about which one is deepest. There's no way to check.
