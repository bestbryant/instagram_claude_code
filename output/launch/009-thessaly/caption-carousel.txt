Imaginary planet No. 009: Thessaly. A planet for people who check the weather app ten times a day.

On Thessaly the rain goes up. It lifts off the sea in thin threads and gathers in shallow pools on the undersides of the clouds, which catch the light at dusk. People wear their umbrellas on their feet and buy boots that are waterproof on top. The weather app has one setting: from below. The pools in the sky all have names, and the argument about which is deepest is three hundred years old.
