Imaginary planet No. 064: Figment. A planet for every parent of a three-year-old.

When a child stops needing them, imaginary friends come to Figment. It's quiet. There are armchairs. Some take up hobbies, but mostly they wait, because every so often one of them gets called back. Barnaby was Freya's, from the age of three until about seven: tall, very polite, never without his red umbrella. He has waited on the same hill ever since. Last week, Freya's daughter turned three. Barnaby was gone by teatime, and back by bedtime.

Names welcome below. I find a few planets each evening.
