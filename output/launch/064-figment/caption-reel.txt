Invented planet No. 064: Figment. A planet for every parent of a three-year-old.

Look for the red umbrella.

Figment: a 28-hour day, no moons, gentle gravity and a lot of armchairs. When a child stops needing an imaginary friend, the friend comes here: a lion who was mostly a dog, a girl who lived behind the sofa, a horse called Steve. They take up hobbies. Mostly they wait, because now and then one gets called back. Barnaby was Freya's, from the age of three till about seven. Tall, polite, never without his umbrella.

One of them recognised me. I'd rather not say from where.

(On Earth, a study at the University of Oregon found that about two children in three have had an imaginary friend by the age of seven. On Figment, a few are still waiting to be asked.)
