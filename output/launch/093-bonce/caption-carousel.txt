Imaginary planet No. 093: Bonce. A planet for whoever's got the brain cell today.

On Bonce, best friends share one brain cell, and you can see who's got it: a small light just above their head. It does the thinking for both of them, which is fine as long as nobody asks anything difficult. Becky has it weekdays, for work. Nic gets weekends. One Saturday night they left it in the back of a taxi, and with nothing to think with between them, they did the only thing that made sense. They woke up on Sunday with a dog. They've called him Kebab.
