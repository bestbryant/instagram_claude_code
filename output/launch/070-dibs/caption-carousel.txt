Imaginary planet No. 070: Dibs. A planet for every big sister who called dibs.

On Dibs, calling dibs is binding for life, so children here learn to talk early and fast. Most claims are small: a window seat, the last roast potato, the good spoon. Kim, when she was tiny, called dibs on sunsets. She meant the one she was looking at. The planet took her at her word, and since then every evening on Dibs has gone straight from afternoon to dark, except wherever Kim happens to be standing. She has never lent one out. Then her little sister got married, on a hill, in the evening, and Kim lent her the sunset. It was the best sunset anyone there had ever seen, mostly because it was the only one. Kim wants it back washed.
