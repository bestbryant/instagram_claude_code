Invented planet No. 070: Dibs. A planet for every big sister who called dibs.

Kim has the only sunset for miles.

Dibs: 5.3 million people, a 23-hour day, one moon (claimed), and a rule every child knows by heart: call dibs and it's yours for life. Most claims are small. A window seat. The last roast potato. The good spoon. Kim, when she was tiny, called dibs on sunsets, and the planet took her at her word. Since then every evening on Dibs has gone straight from afternoon to dark, except wherever Kim happens to be standing. Then her little sister got married, on a hill, in the evening.

I called dibs on a window seat on the ferry. I'm writing this from the ferry.

(The story goes that 'dibs' comes from dibstones, an old children's game played with pebbles or knucklebones. On Dibs, most of the pebbles are spoken for.)

What did you call dibs on first?
