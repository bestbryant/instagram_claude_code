Imaginary planet No. 001: Veyra. A planet for people who always want five more minutes.

Veyra has two suns, and one of them is nearly always up, so the whole night lasts nine minutes. Beds went out of fashion centuries ago. People sleep sitting up in good armchairs, shoes on, ready to go. The lighthouse works nine minutes a night and is widely thought to have the best job going. Hit snooze once and you miss the night entirely.

What would you do with a nine-minute night?
Leave a name in the comments. I chart a few every evening.
