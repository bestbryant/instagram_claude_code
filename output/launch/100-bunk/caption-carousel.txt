Imaginary planet No. 100: Bunk. A planet for every grown man in his old bedroom.

On Bunk, anyone who goes back to their mum's is nine again by the time they're through the door. Not in spirit. Nine: the height, the bedtime, the opinions about crusts. It wears off on the drive home, usually around the services. Gareth's a judge. At Christmas he went home with his brother, and by the doorstep they were both up on their parents' shoulders. He asked for squash. Mum sent them both to bed at eight, and they went. At ten, Gareth asked for a glass of water. He's sentencing someone on Monday.
