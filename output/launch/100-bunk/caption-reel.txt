Invented planet No. 100: Bunk. A planet for every grown man in his old bedroom.

Leave a name: it gets its own planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

They both wiped their feet.

Bunk: 3.4 million people, a 22-hour day, two moons, and a rule about going home: step through your mum's front door, and you're nine again. Not in spirit. Nine. The height, the bedtime, the strong feelings about crusts. It wears off on the drive back, usually around the services.

I only went round to my mum's to borrow a stepladder. I came out with a packed lunch.

(Children of nine need nine to twelve hours' sleep a night, according to the American Academy of Sleep Medicine. On Bunk, so do judges.)

What time would your mum send you up?
