A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 073: Fret. A planet for the family worrier.

That clear sky is about to fill up.

Fret: 1.7 million people, a 27-hour day, and moons that depend on the week. A worry here leaves your head and hangs over your house at whatever size you've made it. A lost key makes a pebble. A hospital letter makes something you could land on. Anna's sky has been full for years, and very little of it is hers, and her friends keep telling her not to.

I worried I'd packed the wrong maps. It followed me round for a week, about the size of a satsuma.

(On Earth, Jupiter is about eleven Earths wide, and as far as anyone knows, it isn't worried about anything.)

How big is yours this week?
