Imaginary planet No. 073: Fret. A planet for the family worrier.

On Fret, a worry doesn't stay in your head. It rises off the roof and hangs over the house, as big as you've made it, where the neighbours can see. Most people have a moon or two. Anna has a gas giant, and hardly any of it is hers: her sister's job interview, the neighbour's hip, a friend's first date. Then that friend told her not to worry. Now it's got rings.
