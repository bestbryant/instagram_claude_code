Imaginary planet No. 060: Clasp. A planet for every mum waiting at arrivals.

On Clasp, a hug lasts exactly as long as you've been apart. Back from the shops: a squeeze. Back from a fortnight in the sun: a fortnight. Zoe went travelling for a year, and her mum got to the airport an hour early, holding flowers. The plane landed. They've been there since June. Zoe's dad brings them sandwiches, and a coat each now the snow's started.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
