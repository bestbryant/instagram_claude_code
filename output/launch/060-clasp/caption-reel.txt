A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 060: Clasp. A planet for every mum waiting at arrivals.

Those two by the sign won't be long.

Clasp: 5.6 million people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and hugs that last exactly as long as you've been apart. Back from the shops: a squeeze. Back from a fortnight away: a fortnight. Gap years are planned very carefully, and airports have benches for the people waiting to be let go of. Zoe went travelling for a year. Her mum got to the airport an hour early, with flowers.

(On Earth, a study of athletes at the 2008 Olympics timed the average hug at about three seconds. On Clasp, that means you nipped out for milk.)

Short trip or long hug?
