Imaginary planet No. 078: Kip. A planet for every new dad who knows one song.

On Kip, a lullaby works on everyone in earshot. Midwives wear earplugs, and nobody hums at the wheel. New parents pick a song, keep it low and shut the windows. Ben only knows one, and sings it to his daughter every night; his street hasn't slept so well in years. At her christening, he hummed it without thinking. The congregation went down like skittles. The baby's wide awake.
