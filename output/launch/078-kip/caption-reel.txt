Invented planet No. 078: Kip. A planet for every new dad who knows one song.

Nobody's asleep yet. Give it a second.

Kip: 1.5 million people, a 31-hour day, one moon, and lullabies that work on anyone who can hear them. Midwives wear earplugs. Nobody hums at the wheel. New parents pick one song and keep it low, because the neighbours have work in the morning. Ben knows exactly one. He sings it to his daughter every night, and his whole street has never looked better.

I hummed something in a hotel lift. We all woke up in the basement.

(On Earth, Harvard researchers found that babies relax to lullabies even in languages they've never heard. Nobody tested the parents.)

Which song got you to sleep?
