Imaginary planet No. 056: Kith. A planet for every best friend who moved away.

On Kith, houses follow your best friend. Sophie and Hannah grew up next door to each other and stayed there for decades. Then Sophie moved to the sea. Hannah's house went after her the same afternoon, across two counties and a motorway, and Hannah was in the bath at the time. It settled on the cliff next to Sophie's, a little crooked, with the kettle still warm. Nobody told Hannah's husband.
