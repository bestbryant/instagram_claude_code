Invented planet No. 056: Kith. A planet for every best friend who moved away.

Only one of those houses was there yesterday.

Kith: 1.6 million people, a 23-hour day, no moons, and houses that follow your best friend. Estate agents ask about your friendships before your budget, and removal vans are mostly for pianos. Sophie and Hannah grew up next door to each other. When Sophie moved to the coast, Hannah's house went the same afternoon and settled beside hers, a little crooked, with the bath still running.

(On Earth, hermit crabs swap shells in a queue: when a bigger one washes up, they line up in order of size and everybody moves house at once. On Kith, nobody checks who's in the bath.)

Is your best friend in the same postcode?
