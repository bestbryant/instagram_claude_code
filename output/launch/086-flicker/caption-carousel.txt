Imaginary planet No. 086: Flicker. A planet for anyone who took ages to make a move.

On Flicker, the sea lights up whenever anybody, anywhere along the coast, has their first kiss. Most nights it flickers a bit. School discos are visible from space. Fishermen have learned to read it like a newspaper. Matt walked Holly home along the front every Friday for a year, and every Friday the sea stayed dark, and the whole town noticed. Then one Friday, at the end of the pier, he finally went for it. The bay lit up from end to end. Her dad was out fishing.
