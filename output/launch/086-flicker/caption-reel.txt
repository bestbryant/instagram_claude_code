A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 086: Flicker. A planet for anyone who took ages to make a move.

Holly lives in that little house.

Flicker: 410,000 people, a 22-hour day, one moon that's rarely needed, and a sea that lights up for every first kiss on the coast. Most nights it flickers a bit. Fishermen read it like a newspaper, and the harbour master keeps a chart. Matt walked Holly home along the front every Friday for a year. Every Friday, the sea stayed dark, and the whole town noticed.

I stood on the pier for an hour to see it. The sea gave me one dim flicker, out of pity.

(On Earth, some bays glow blue at night: a plankton called sea sparkle lights up whenever the water's disturbed. Nobody has to kiss anyone.)

How long did yours take to make a move?
