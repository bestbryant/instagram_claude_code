Two new planets a day. Leave a name in the comments and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 081: Ditto. A planet for husbands who wander off in shops.

Spot Gwen's husband. Take your time.

Ditto: 5.8 million people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and not one face you could pick out of a crowd. Everyone is the same height, wears the same coat and walks the same walk. Couples hold hands in public, less for romance than for admin, and wedding photographers charge extra. Gwen let go of her husband for a moment at the garden centre, between the bird baths and the bedding plants.

I waved at my guide across the market. I now have eleven guides.

(On Earth, about one person in fifty has face blindness, and knows their family by a walk, a voice or a coat. On Ditto it's everyone, and the coats don't help.)

How would you find yours in a crowd?
