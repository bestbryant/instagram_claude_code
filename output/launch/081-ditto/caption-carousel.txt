Imaginary planet No. 081: Ditto. A planet for husbands who wander off in shops.

On Ditto, everyone looks exactly the same: the same height, the same coat, the same walk. Couples hold hands in public, less for romance than for admin. Gwen let go of her husband for a moment at the garden centre, somewhere between the bird baths and the bedding plants, and when she looked up he was one of forty. She waved. Everyone waved back, except one man, who had his hands full. He'd gone to get her flowers.
