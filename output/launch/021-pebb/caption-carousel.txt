Imaginary planet No. 021: Pebb. A planet for people who've just moved somewhere quiet.

Pebb is a rock about forty kilometres across, small enough that a good throw goes into orbit and comes home two hours later from the other side. Juno lives there alone, she thinks. Every morning she throws her ball east, has a long breakfast, and catches it coming in from the west. One morning it came home wet. The next, wet and chewed. The next, she threw two.
