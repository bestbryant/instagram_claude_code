A planet for people who've just moved somewhere quiet. (Imaginary planet No. 021: Pebb)

That's no moon in the first frame.

Pebb is a rock about forty kilometres across: one house, one resident, and a horizon you could walk to before lunch. Juno moved here for the quiet. Every morning she throws her ball east, has a long breakfast, and turns round to catch it coming in over the roof. She has never walked round to the far side. It isn't far.

I threw my compass on my first morning, to check. It's still up there, next to her first ball.

On a rock this size, a throw of about 18 metres a second goes into orbit and comes round again in just under two hours. On Earth, you'd need nearly 8 kilometres a second.

Would you throw two?
