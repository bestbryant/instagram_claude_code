Imaginary planet No. 069: Likeness. A planet for every mum who's never in the photos.

On Likeness, a photo shows whoever took it. Point a camera at a waterfall and you get a nice one of yourself, squinting. Selfies are just photos. So most family albums are just whoever did the organising: Mum at the beach, Mum at the zoo, Mum at every school play, mid-sentence. This year the family decided she should have one of her own, so Dad took it. It's a lovely one of Dad. He's holding a fish.

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
