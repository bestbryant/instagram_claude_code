Invented planet No. 069: Likeness. A planet for every mum who's never in the photos.

Four of them posed for this one.

Likeness: 3.8 million people, a 25-hour day, no moons, and cameras that only ever capture whoever's holding them. Point one at a waterfall and you get a nice one of yourself, squinting. Selfies are just photos. So most family albums are just whoever did the organising: Mum at the beach, Mum at the zoo, Mum at every school play, mid-sentence. This year, for once, Dad took one of her.

I photographed the harbour for my notes. I look tired.

(On Earth, one of the oldest surviving photographs of a person is a self-portrait: Robert Cornelius, Philadelphia, 1839, who had to keep still for several minutes while the plate exposed. On Likeness, every photo is one.)

Who's behind the camera in your family?
