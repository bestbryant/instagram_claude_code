Invented planet No. 028: Whisk. A planet for parents who find their tea in the microwave.

Wren's cup has been going cold since Monday.

Whisk spins so fast that a day lasts six minutes. A year is over in a day and a half, so birthdays are relentless, lunch is largely theoretical and every calendar is a flipbook. Wren has twins, a kettle and one small ambition. The twins have other plans, and very good timing.

I tried keeping a diary. By the time I'd written the date, it was wrong.

(On Earth, the day gets about 1.8 thousandths of a second longer every century, as the tides drag on us. It won't help.)

When did you last finish a hot drink?
