A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 046: Yonder. A planet for every nan who's still waving.

Bottom left: Nan, mid-wave.

Yonder: 56,000 people, a 28-hour day, no moons, and not one hill. The planet is flat from pole to pole and the air is perfectly clear, so nothing ever goes out of sight. You can watch a friend walk home for days. Lighthouses are mainly ornamental, and hide-and-seek never caught on. When the family leaves after Sunday lunch, Nan stands at the gate and waves until they're out of sight, which takes about four days.

I waved back for the first mile. My arm gave out at mile four.

(On Earth, stand on a beach and the horizon is only about five kilometres away. Every nan on Earth has been waving at a curve.)

Who waves you off?
