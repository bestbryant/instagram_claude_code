Imaginary planet No. 046: Yonder. A planet for every nan who's still waving.

Yonder is flat from pole to pole, with no hills, no bends and no weather to speak of, and the air is so clear that nothing ever goes out of sight. You can watch a friend walk home for days. Lighthouses are mainly ornamental. After Sunday lunch, Nan stands at the gate and waves you off until you're out of sight, the way nans do. On Yonder, that takes four days. You wave back for the first mile. When you get home, she's still waving. Then she phones to check you got back all right.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
