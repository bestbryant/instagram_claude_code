Any name you leave gets a planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Invented planet No. 102: Crumb. A planet for every dad who ate the mince pie.

The sherry was a trap too.

Crumb: 5.2 million people, a 24-hour day, one moon, and one rule on Christmas Eve: whoever eats the mince pie left out for Santa becomes Santa until morning. Every chimney on the planet, in whatever they had on. Most dads find out around eleven, with crumbs down their jumper.

I spent a Christmas Eve with a family there. I tidied up the plate before bed, to be helpful. I did the whole southern hemisphere.

(The story goes that British children leave Father Christmas a mince pie and a glass of sherry, and a carrot for the reindeer. On Crumb, the carrot's the only safe thing on the plate.)

What would your kids open first?
