Imaginary planet No. 102: Crumb. A planet for every dad who ate the mince pie.

On Crumb, whoever eats the mince pie left out for Santa becomes Santa until morning. Every chimney on the planet, in whatever they had on. Most dads find out around eleven, with crumbs down their jumper. This year, the kids left out two, and Mum had the other one. The kids had planned it since October. By the time their parents got back down, they'd opened everything. Even Nan's.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
