Imaginary planet No. 095: Brew. A planet for flatmates who only come out for tea.

On Brew, putting the kettle on summons everyone you live with. Flatmates simply appear round you, mugs out, from wherever they were and whatever they were doing. Abbie's flatmates never leave their rooms. She knows them mostly by their microwaves. One night she boiled some for a hot water bottle, just for herself. Nine came. She has four flatmates. One was the landlord, with paperwork. She's bought a coffee machine.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
