Invented planet No. 095: Brew. A planet for flatmates who only come out for tea.

Nobody hears a kettle like a flatmate.

Brew: 2.4 million people, a 25-hour day, two moons, and kettles that summon everyone you live with. Put one on, and your flatmates appear round you with their mugs out, wherever they were and whatever they were doing. Kitchens here are small and very full. Nobody on Brew has ever made a quiet cup of tea.

I rented a room there for a month. I met my flatmates once, at the kettle, every forty minutes.

(In Britain, so many people put the kettle on at the end of a big match on telly that the National Grid plans for it. They call it TV pickup. On Brew, it's called Tuesday.)

How many mugs would you need at yours?
