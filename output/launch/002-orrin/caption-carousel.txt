Imaginary planet No. 002: Orrin. A planet for people who pack the night before.

The giant that Orrin orbits pulls its ocean away for eleven years at a time. Towns go up on the dry seabed in between: houses, a school, a very optimistic swimming pool. The date the water comes back is printed on every calendar. Everyone still packs the night before, plank by plank, arguing about the mugs. My guide had moved twice. The second time, she brought a towel.
