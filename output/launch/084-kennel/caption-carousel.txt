Imaginary planet No. 084: Kennel. A planet for whoever forgot the anniversary.

On Kennel, the doghouse is the moon. Forget a birthday or an anniversary and you spend the night up there, with a camp bed, a kettle and the best view in the system. On a bad night, half the street is up there. Dean forgot his and Emma's anniversary, and up he went. At midnight, Emma went up too. She took the cake. Best anniversary they've had.
