Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 084: Kennel. A planet for whoever forgot the anniversary.

There's a spare room in that moon.

Kennel: 1.2 million people, a 25-hour day, one very large moon, and nowhere on the ground to sulk. Forget a birthday or an anniversary here and you spend the night on the moon. It's not unpleasant: there's a camp bed, a kettle and the best view in the system, and the lift runs late. On a bad night, half the street is up there. Dean forgot his and Emma's anniversary.

I missed my editor's birthday. The moon is lovely this time of year.

(The story goes that 'in the doghouse' owes a lot to Peter Pan, where Mr Darling moves into the dog's kennel to make amends. On Kennel, the kennel has a view.)

What would get you sent to the moon?
