Imaginary planet No. 052: Ajar. A planet for every friend who comes round anyway.

On Ajar, front doors say how the person inside is feeling. It saves a lot of knocking. Since her break-up, Amy's door has said the same thing every day, and her friends have done as it says. Her best friend Lou has never once done what a door told her. She came round with crisps, the good ones. The door changed its mind: GLAD YOU CAME.
