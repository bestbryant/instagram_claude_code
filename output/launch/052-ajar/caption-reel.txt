Invented planet No. 052: Ajar. A planet for every friend who comes round anyway.

Read the door before you knock.

Ajar: 640,000 people, a 22-hour day, one moon, and front doors that say how the person inside is feeling. Nobody knocks. Postmen read the door and decide. Most say COME IN, or BACK SOON, or, by mid-December, NOT ANOTHER CARD. Amy's has said the same thing since the break-up, and everyone has been very respectful about it, apart from Lou.

My hotel door said LOST all week. Housekeeping kept leaving me maps.

(The story goes that in the Netherlands, when a teenager passes their final exams, the family hangs a school bag from the flagpole. On Ajar, the door would say PASSED, and then, quietly, FINALLY.)

What's your front door saying right now?
