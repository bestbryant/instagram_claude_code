Imaginary planet No. 048: Brolly. A planet for every mum who says it'll brighten up.

On Brolly, it rains on the beach until the last person goes home, and then the sun comes out. Every time. Families arrive at nine with windbreaks, a flask and a mum who says it'll brighten up later, and they sit under their umbrellas until teatime, eating damp sandwiches and watching the rain land on the sea. As the last family leaves, the clouds part, and the beach is gorgeous for nobody. Every family books again for next year.

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
