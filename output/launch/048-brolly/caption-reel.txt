Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 048: Brolly. A planet for every mum who says it'll brighten up.

Mum's the one pointing at the sky.

Brolly: 3.1 million people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and the best beaches in the atlas, if anyone could ever see them. It rains on the sand until the last person leaves, and then the sun comes out. Every time. Families arrive at nine with windbreaks, a flask and a mum who has seen the forecast and chosen not to believe it. They eat their sandwiches under umbrellas and watch the rain land on the sea.

I stayed till dark to prove a point. So did the rain.

(On Earth, a big raindrop lands at about nine metres a second, and drizzle drifts down at under two. Mum calls both 'spitting'.)

What does your mum always say on holiday?
