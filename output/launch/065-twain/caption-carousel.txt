Imaginary planet No. 065: Twain. A planet for the cousin you see every summer.

Twain's gravity is weak and the neighbouring planet hangs just above the hills, so with a good run-up you can jump across. Families save it for the summer holidays and pack for months. The jump takes about a minute, and everybody screams the whole way. Ola is from down here; her cousin Remi is from up there. Every summer since they were small, they have both jumped, met halfway and picked up exactly where they left off. This time they both had children on their shoulders. Same jump. Smaller cousins.
