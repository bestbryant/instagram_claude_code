Invented planet No. 065: Twain. A planet for the cousin you see every summer.

Watch the sky behind the hill.

Twain: 4.3 million people, a 25-hour day, one moon and a neighbour you can jump to, with a run-up. Most families go in the summer holidays. They pack for months, the jump takes about a minute, and everyone screams the whole way. Ola is from down here; her cousin Remi is from up there. Every summer since they were small, they've jumped, met halfway and picked up exactly where they left off.

I jumped too. I landed in a stranger's garden party. They gave me a plate.

(On the Moon, gravity is about a sixth of Earth's, so the same jump goes about six times as high. On Twain, it goes all the way to your cousin's.)

Could you make that jump?
