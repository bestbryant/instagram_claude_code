Invented planet No. 042: Fond. A planet for people who hear 'Take a coat' and know what it means.

Even the station sign is saying it.

Fond is 880,000 people, long winters and a great deal of knitwear. Here, 'I love you' works once: say it a second time and it comes out as a cough. So everyone keeps theirs, and the language has filled up with stand-ins: 'Mind the step', 'Ring your nan', 'There's lasagne in the freezer'. Teenagers find all of it unbearable, then grow up and say every word.

Eleven strangers told me to take a coat on my first day. I've never felt so loved.

(The story goes that plenty of Earth families never say it out loud either. In Chinese, asking whether you've eaten has been doing the job for generations. On Fond, it's mostly coats.)

What's your family's 'Take a coat'?
