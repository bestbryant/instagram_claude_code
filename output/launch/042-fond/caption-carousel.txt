Imaginary planet No. 042: Fond. A planet for people who hear 'Take a coat' and know what it means.

On Fond, 'I love you' only works once, so nobody wastes it. Instead they say 'Take a coat', or 'Text me when you're home'. Kath spent hers on Sam before he could talk. He doesn't remember it. She's said 'Take a coat' every day since. Last winter, at the station, Sam left for the city, and spent his on her. She said, 'Take a coat.'
