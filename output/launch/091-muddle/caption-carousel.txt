Imaginary planet No. 091: Muddle. A planet for everyone Mum calls by the dog's name.

On Muddle, you turn into whatever your mum calls you. Get the dog's name by mistake and you're the dog until she sorts it out, which in most houses is after the washing-up. Sunday dinner is the worst of it. Pickle is the family spaniel, and one Sunday Mum called Shaun Pickle, and Shaun was a spaniel. By pudding she'd done all three of her boys. Then Dad offered to help. She was left at the table with a spoon and five spaniels, all sitting very nicely and looking at her. First time they've come when called.
