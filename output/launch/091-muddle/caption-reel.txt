Invented planet No. 091: Muddle. A planet for everyone Mum calls by the dog's name.

Count the spaniels. Then count the sons.

Muddle: 2.3 million people, a 25-hour day, two moons, and mums whose mix-ups come true. Get the wrong name from your mum and you turn into whoever she said, until she gets it right, which in most houses is after the washing-up. Sunday dinner is the worst of it. Pickle is the spaniel. By pudding, all three boys were Pickle too.

I stayed with a family there. Mum kept calling me Nathan, her youngest. I spent the weekend nine years old and was sent to bed at eight.

(In 2016, psychologists at Duke University found that people mix up names within a group, family with family, and that the dog's name gets pulled in, but rarely the cat's. On Muddle, that's a lot of spaniels.)

How many names does your mum try before she gets to yours?
