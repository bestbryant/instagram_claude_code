Imaginary planet No. 076: Thaw. A planet for anyone who'd talk to a lamppost.

On Thaw, every party starts frozen. Guests arrive, take a drink, stand round the edges, and the snow comes in. Nobody can move until somebody breaks the ice. At Kev's thirtieth it was nearly midnight before Meera arrived and told a stranger she loved his shoes. It was spring by the sausage rolls.

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
