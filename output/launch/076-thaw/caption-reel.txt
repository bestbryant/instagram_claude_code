Invented planet No. 076: Thaw. A planet for anyone who'd talk to a lamppost.

Blink and you'll miss the warm bit.

Thaw: 720,000 people, a 29-hour day, two moons, and a climate that runs entirely on small talk. Every gathering here starts in deep winter: weddings, barbecues, the office do. Hosts lay on blankets and hot drinks, and wait. Everyone has a friend like Meera, who can thaw a room with one question about the dog. She is invited to absolutely everything.

I tried 'Lovely weather.' Six inches, overnight.

(The story goes that 'breaking the ice' first meant clearing a frozen river so the boats could get through. By the 1600s, it meant getting strangers talking.)

What's your line for breaking the ice?
