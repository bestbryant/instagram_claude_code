Imaginary planet No. 000: Elsewhere. Every name has a planet. Yours too.

Each day I fill one in, sometimes with a world I came across on my own, more often with one somebody asked me to find. If you leave a name in the comments, any name at all, I'll go looking for the planet that goes with it.
