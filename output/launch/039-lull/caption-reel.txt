Invented planet No. 039: Lull. A planet for people who stopped being the baby of the family.

Guess whose moon that is.

Lull is 290 people, low hills and one moon, which follows the youngest person on the planet and waits over their roof all night. New parents never buy a night light. When the moon moves house, the whole planet knows by breakfast, and somebody takes round a cake. It hadn't moved for six years.

I tried to read by it on my first night. It was over a farm two valleys away.

(On Earth, the moon seems to chase the car because it's about 384,000 kilometres away, so a whole drive barely shifts it across the sky. On Lull, it really is following.)

Did you sulk when the baby came?
