Imaginary planet No. 039: Lull. A planet for people who stopped being the baby of the family.

On Lull, the moon follows the youngest person on the planet and waits over their roof all night. When there's a new baby, it moves house, so gossip is mostly astronomy. For six years it sat over Alfie's. Then his sister was born, at home, and it didn't move an inch. It just wasn't his any more. Alfie took it badly, for about a week. Now he shows her where it is. She's usually asleep.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
