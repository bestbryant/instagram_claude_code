A planet for the friend who remembers every birthday. (Imaginary planet No. 019: Tessel)

Somebody keeps that board up to date.

Tessel is 90,000 people on a slow, tilted world: winter lasts a year, and summer about a fortnight. With no sun to count by, every village has someone who keeps the date. Nobody applies. The village just notices who has been doing it. Jess has kept hers since she was a girl: days, birthdays, anniversaries, and the morning the goats came in.

I dated my first page 'winter'. Jess crossed it out and wrote 212.

In Longyearbyen, on Svalbard, the sun doesn't reach the town from late October until early March, and there's a festival week for the day it first touches the steps of the old hospital. On Tessel, they wait a year, and somebody counts.

Could you count a whole winter in chalk?
