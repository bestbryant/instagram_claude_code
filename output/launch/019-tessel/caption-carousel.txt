Imaginary planet No. 019: Tessel. A planet for the friend who remembers every birthday.

Winter on Tessel lasts a year, with no sun and no calendar, so every village needs someone who knows what day it is. Jess keeps the count on a board by her door. She knows every birthday, even the goats'. She's paid in the first slice, and hasn't bought cake in years. One winter, Jess lost count. The village hadn't. On Tessel they say: for the cold, firewood. For the birthdays, a jess.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
