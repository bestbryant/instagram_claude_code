A planet for people who still write letters. (Imaginary planet No. 006: Luma)

Luma's moon comes round every October, low enough to clip the hilltops. You hear it a day before you see it: a hum in your back teeth. Every dog on the planet lies down.

A letter pressed to the moon sticks for the whole orbit. Be polite: it has answered rude ones. The eleven-page reply ended with a drawing of a dog, done from description. It was mostly ears. So is Biscuit, Frank's eighth dog and the first to get post.

It has been late once, in 1987. It won't say where it went.

Our own moon drifts 3.8 cm further away every year. Luma's keeps coming back.

I went up this year and wrote about a cat called Everything. Nothing yet.

What would you tell the moon about your dog?
