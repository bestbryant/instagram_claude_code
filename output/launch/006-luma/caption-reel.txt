Once a year, Luma's moon comes in so close that it hums, a low note you feel in your teeth. Children climb the hill with letters and press them against its surface as it drifts past. Most of the letters are never seen again. Every few years one comes back the following autumn, answered, in handwriting nobody recognises. Those ones get framed.
