Imaginary planet No. 006: Luma. A planet for people who still write letters.

Once a year Luma's moon comes in so close that it hums, a low note you feel in your teeth. Children climb the hill with letters and press them against it as it drifts past. Most are never seen again. Every few years one comes back the following autumn, answered, in handwriting no one recognises. The longest reply so far ran to four pages and was mostly questions about dogs.

What would you write on yours?
Names welcome below. I find a few planets each evening.
