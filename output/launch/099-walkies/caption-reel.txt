Invented planet No. 099: Walkies. A planet for everyone with a spaniel.

Every name has a planet. Leave yours.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Pip has done this walk before.

Walkies: 2.1 million people, about four million dogs, a 25-hour day, two moons, and a dog walk that goes as far as the dog would like: three deserts, a red one, a stony one and a frozen one, then home for tea. Dogs do it before lunch. People take a flask, spare socks and, if they're sensible, the bus fare back.

I borrowed a spaniel for the afternoon. I got as far as the stony one, and she sent a postcard.

(The largest desert on Earth is Antarctica. A desert is about how little falls from the sky, not how hot it is. On Walkies, it's the spaniels' favourite.)

How many deserts would yours do before tea?
