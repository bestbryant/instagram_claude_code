Imaginary planet No. 099: Walkies. A planet for everyone with a spaniel.

On Walkies, a dog walk goes as far as the dog would like, and it would like three deserts. A red one, a stony one and a frozen one, then home for tea. Dogs do it before lunch. People take a flask. Ian took Pip, his spaniel, out after breakfast. In the third desert, Ian sat down in the snow for a minute, and Pip carried on. Pip was home in time for tea, and had it. Ian's still walking back.
