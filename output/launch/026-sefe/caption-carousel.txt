Imaginary planet No. 026: Sefe. A planet for people who keep every nice thing anyone's said about them.

On Sefe nothing shines: not water, not glass, not eyes. So nobody sees their own face, and other people have to tell you what it looks like. Lovers trade descriptions on the first night, mothers go first, and barbers finish every haircut with a long, careful one. Oskar's wife died in the winter. He still comes in every month, and the barber doesn't say a word.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
