Imaginary planet No. 034: Lode. A planet for people who go home for Christmas.

Everyone on Lode is born with a little iron in the blood, and it points to where they were born. Every December, the whole planet follows it home. Most of them were born in the hospital, which is a supermarket now, so every December thirty thousand people come home to aisle three. Sister Bridget delivered every one of them. She's on the tills now.
