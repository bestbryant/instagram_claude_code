Invented planet No. 034: Lode. A planet for people who go home for Christmas.

The whole queue was born in that shop.

Lode is a snowy, hilly planet of 52,000 people, and none of them has ever been lost. You're born with a little iron in the blood, and it points, very gently, at the spot where you arrived. Sat-navs never caught on. In December the whole planet sets off home, and most of it ends up at the big supermarket on the ring road, which was the hospital until 1988. Midwives on Lode don't really retire.

The man on the next till asked where mine pointed. I said down. He went very quiet and gave me a free bag.

(You have iron in your blood too: three or four grams of it, most of it in your red cells. Ours doesn't point anywhere. We go home anyway.)

Where does your iron point?
