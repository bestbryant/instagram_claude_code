Imaginary planet No. 023: Sooner. A planet for people who keep saying 'we should catch up'.

On Sooner, 'sometime' is an actual date. It comes round every forty years, and every 'we should get coffee sometime' anyone has ever said falls due that morning. So does every 'let's do this again' said after a bad first date; those tables finish early. For the first hour, nobody knows what to say. Then nobody else leaves early, and nobody says 'sometime' on the way out, because that gets you another forty years. They say 'Saturday'.
