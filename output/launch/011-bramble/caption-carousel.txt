Imaginary planet No. 011: Bramble. A planet for people who never come downstairs.

Trees on Bramble don't stop growing. The oldest are taller than the hills they stand on, and the tallest has a small cloud in its upper branches that rains on that tree and nowhere else. Somewhere near the top is a village that hasn't come down in four hundred years. They drop pinecones with notes tied on. The ground answers by kite, and complains about the noise.

Would you move up, or stay on the ground?
