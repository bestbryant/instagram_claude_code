Imaginary planet No. 010: Kess. A planet for people who stopped noticing the view from their window.

The aurora on Kess has been on since before anyone wrote things down, so the people who live there stopped looking up. Tourists lie down in the middle of the road to watch it. Commuters step around them without breaking stride. Now and then one of the commuters looks up by accident and has to sit down on the kerb for a minute.

What did you stop noticing where you live?
