Kess

The aurora on Kess has been on for as long as anyone has kept records, so the people who live there stopped looking up a long time ago. Visitors lie down in the middle of the road to watch it. Locals step around them on the way to work. Every so often a local looks up by accident and has to sit down on the kerb for a minute.
