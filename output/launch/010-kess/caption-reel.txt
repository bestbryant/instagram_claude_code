Invented planet No. 010: Kess. A planet for people who stopped noticing the view from their window.

Watch the man walking on the right.

Kess: 250,000 people, a 21-hour day, no moons, and an aurora bright enough to cast shadows. A local who looks up at it by accident has to sit on the kerb for a minute. The night it went out, every kerb on Kess was full. I was lying in the left lane outside the laundrette, where the road stays warm. For four minutes, nobody stepped round me.

Nobody knows why it went out. The council put up a plaque about it at eye level, so nobody has read it. The buses now allow three minutes per local.

(On Earth, the brightest aurora lights the ground about as well as a full moon, and a full moon cuts the stars you can see from a few thousand to a few hundred.)

What did you stop noticing where you live?
