Imaginary planet No. 059: Thicket. A planet for every husband who'll get round to it.

On Thicket, saying 'later' plants a tree. Most gardens have a couple. Phil has been going to fix the gate since the kids were little, and the house now stands in a small wood. Then his wife asked about the gate again. 'Later,' he said. The house hasn't been seen since, though the kitchen light is still on in there somewhere. He'll clear the wood. Later.
