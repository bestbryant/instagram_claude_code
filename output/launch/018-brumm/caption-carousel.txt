Imaginary planet No. 018: Brumm. A planet for everyone who was carried home asleep.

Gravity on Brumm is twice ours, so the hills are low, the houses are one storey and nobody owns a ladder. By four, a child is too heavy to carry far. So, in the week they get too heavy, every child gets one Last Carry: the street closes the road, the neighbours come out to cheer, and a parent carries them wherever they choose. Most choose the bakery. Every child on Brumm is asleep by the end.
