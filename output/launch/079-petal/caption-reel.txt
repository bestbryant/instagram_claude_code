Leave any name. I'll find its planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Invented planet No. 079: Petal. A planet for couples with embarrassing pet names.

The hills behind Jim are empty. For now.

Petal: 740,000 people, a 23-hour day, one moon, and pet names that are taken literally. Call someone 'sunshine' and out it comes. 'Angel' is a lot of feathers. Most couples play it safe with 'love', and nobody says 'my rock' twice. Jim has called Linda 'sunshine' every morning for twenty years, and their street grows the best tomatoes in the county. Linda calls Jim something else.

I called a bus driver 'duck'. We waited forty minutes for them to cross.

(The story goes that 'honey' has been a pet name since the 1300s. On Petal, the bees have been a problem for about as long.)

Sunshine, pumpkin, or something worse?
