Imaginary planet No. 079: Petal. A planet for couples with embarrassing pet names.

On Petal, pet names are taken literally. Call someone 'sunshine' and out comes the sun; call him 'my rock' and you'll want to stand well back. Most couples play it safe with 'love'. Jim has called Linda 'sunshine' every morning for twenty years, and their street has the best tomatoes in the county. Linda calls Jim 'pumpkin'. Every time she says it, the hills fill up with pumpkins. He's never asked her to stop.
