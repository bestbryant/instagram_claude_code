Imaginary planet No. 090: Vow. A planet for people who'd marry them again.

On Vow, everything else disappears at a wedding. The moment the couple turn to face each other, the guests go, then the chapel, then the hills, and there's nothing left on the planet but the two of them. Everyone comes back afterwards, usually somewhere near the cake. The vicar has stopped taking it personally. Carol and Ron got married on Vow fifty years ago, and this spring they renewed their vows, mostly for the photos and partly to see. It still worked. She'd had her hair done.
