Invented planet No. 090: Vow. A planet for people who'd marry them again.

Enjoy the guests while they last.

Vow: 760,000 people, a 23-hour day, one moon, and weddings where everything else disappears. The moment the couple turn to face each other, the guests go, then the chapel, then the hills, and for a minute there's nothing on the planet but the two of them. Everyone comes back afterwards, usually somewhere near the cake. The vicar has stopped taking it personally.

I went to a wedding there. I disappeared at twenty past two and turned up in somebody else's wedding photos.

(The story goes that the bridal veil began as a way to keep the groom from seeing the bride's face until the vows were said. On Vow, it's the groom who can't see anything else.)

Would everything still disappear at yours?
