Invented planet No. 030: Sedge. A planet for everyone who's working while everyone else is out.

Not everyone is up that hill.

Sedge and its moon go round each other the long way. Once in a lifetime the moon comes close enough to see its towns: orchards, washing lines, a great many people waving. Sedge practises for weeks. On the night, every light on the planet goes off so the moon can see the hill, and everyone climbs it with a flask and the children.

I waved until my arm went numb. In fairness to the moon, I wasn't moving.

(On Earth, our own moon drifts about 3.8 centimetres further away every year, and nobody waves it off.)

Have you ever worked the night everyone else was out?
