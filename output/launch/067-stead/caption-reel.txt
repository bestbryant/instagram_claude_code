Invented planet No. 067: Stead. A planet for every dad who didn't have to be.

Mia's up on her mum's shoulders.

Stead: 1.1 million people, a 26-hour day, and one moon for every parent. It comes up over the house on the day you become one and never leaves, so you can read a family off the sky: two moons, mostly, sometimes one, now and then four. Mia had two. Then her mum married Neil, who arrived with a toolbox, a very old car and no moon at all. He never mentioned it. He just did the school run.

I admired the moon over the house next door. It was her ex-husband's. They're civil about it.

(On Earth, the Moon looks the same size as the Sun because it's about four hundred times smaller and about four hundred times closer. On Stead, the closest moon always looks the biggest.)

How many moons would be over your house?
