Imaginary planet No. 067: Stead. A planet for every dad who didn't have to be.

On Stead, every parent gets a moon. It comes up over the house on the day they become one and stays for good, so you can read a family off the sky: two moons, mostly, sometimes one, now and then four. Mia had two. Then her mum married Neil, who arrived with a toolbox, a very old car and no moon at all. He never mentioned it. He did the school run, learned her friends' names and lost at every board game. Then, one teatime, with her mouth full, Mia said, 'Pass the gravy, Dad.' A third moon came up behind the house, closer than the other two. He's still holding the gravy.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
