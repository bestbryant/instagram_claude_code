Imaginary planet No. 054: Umber. A planet for people who keep saying they'll visit more.

On Umber, your shadow keeps your promises. Say you'll be there early, and your shadow is, lying politely on the doormat. Say you'll visit your mum more, and it does, every Sunday, while you're busy. She tells it everything. It never looks at its phone. For once, you tag along. Mum opens the door: 'Hello, stranger.'

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
