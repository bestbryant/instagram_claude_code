Invented planet No. 054: Umber. A planet for people who keep saying they'll visit more.

Nobody is casting that shadow.

Umber: 4.8 million people, a 31-hour day, no moons, and long, low, honest light. Shadows here keep their owners' promises. Say you'll pop round and your shadow pops round. Say you'll ring more and it sits by the phone at the other end, looking hopeful. Mothers on Umber see a great deal of shadow. They make it tea and tell it everything, and it never once checks the time.

I promised my editor the map tomorrow. My shadow has been sitting outside her office since yesterday.

(On Earth, when the sun is about six degrees above the horizon, your shadow is about nine and a half times as long as you are tall. It still won't visit anyone for you.)

Where's your shadow been this week?
