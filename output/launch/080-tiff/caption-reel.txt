Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Invented planet No. 080: Tiff. A planet for the one who's always right.

Check the board beside Jan and Derek.

Tiff: 2.5 million people, a 25-hour day, two moons, and a board beside every couple that names the official winner of each argument as it happens. The washing-up. The bins. Whether that was a fox. Most couples swap back and forth, which keeps things civil. At Jan and Derek's, it hasn't changed since the wedding.

I argued with a waiter about how long to brew tea. A board went up before the kettle boiled.

(On Earth, the psychologist John Gottman found that about two-thirds of couples' arguments are about problems that never get solved. On Tiff, at least somebody wins.)
