Imaginary planet No. 080: Tiff. A planet for the one who's always right.

On Tiff, every couple has a board that settles their arguments: the bins, the satnav, whether that was a fox. The board names the winner, officially. Most couples swap back and forth, which keeps things civil. At Jan and Derek's, it has said the same thing since the wedding. For his sixtieth, Jan lost one on purpose. He's had mugs made.
