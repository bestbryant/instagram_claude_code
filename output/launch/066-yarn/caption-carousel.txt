Imaginary planet No. 066: Yarn. A planet for whoever did all the voices.

On Yarn, a story read aloud happens overhead, above whoever is reading it: the dragon, the pirate ship, the very polite whale. Parents choose carefully, and nobody reads anything with a volcano in it. Lucy's dad read her the whale one every night when she was small and did all the voices, including the whale's, which was Scottish for reasons he has never explained. His eyes aren't so good now, so on Sunday afternoons she reads it to him. The whale still comes. He still does the voices.
