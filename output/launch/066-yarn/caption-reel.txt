Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 066: Yarn. A planet for whoever did all the voices.

That whale is raising its hat.

Yarn: 2.2 million people, a 30-hour day, no moons, and stories that happen in the sky above whoever is reading them aloud. Dragons are discouraged. Pirates are allowed at weekends. Nobody reads anything with a volcano in it. Lucy's dad read her the whale one every night when she was small and did all the voices. The whale's was Scottish, for reasons he has never explained.

I read my map aloud one afternoon. A small, confused coastline hung over the hotel till teatime.

(On Earth, a humpback whale's song can last twenty minutes, and the whale will sing it over and over for hours. Most dads manage one chapter.)

Did yours do all the voices?
