Invented planet No. 097: Peep. A planet for every toddler who thinks you can't see them.

Leave any name. I'll find its planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Keep an eye on Dad.

Peep: 1.1 million people, a 22-hour day, one moon, and peekaboo that really works. Cover your eyes and you're gone; uncover them and you're back, usually where you left yourself. Babies here run the house. Parents spend whole afternoons blinking in and out of the garden, and grandparents are advised to sit down first.

I played it once with a baby on a train. I came back two stops later.

(Child psychologists reckon babies only work out that things still exist when they can't see them at somewhere around eight months. It's called object permanence. On Peep, it's optional.)

How long could you keep it up?
