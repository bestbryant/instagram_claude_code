Imaginary planet No. 097: Peep. A planet for every toddler who thinks you can't see them.

On Peep, peekaboo really works. Cover your eyes and you're gone; uncover them and you're back, usually exactly where you were. Babies here run the house. Maisie's dad did it all afternoon, in and out of the garden, until he fell asleep sitting up. Then Maisie covered her eyes. Mum looked everywhere: the shed, the car, under the sofa, twice. Maisie was on her shoulders the whole time. She's a teenager now. She reappears for lifts.

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
