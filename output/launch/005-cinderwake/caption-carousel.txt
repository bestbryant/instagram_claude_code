Cinderwake

The ground on Cinderwake is warm all year, and so is the rain. At night the canyon walls give off a dull orange light, enough to read by if you sit close. Tea grows wild in the cracks and people leave their kettles out on the rock to boil. Before I went, I was told to bring a cup and not much else. That turned out to be good advice.
