Imaginary planet No. 005: Cinderwake. A planet for people who are always cold.

The ground on Cinderwake is warm all year, and so is the rain. At night the canyon walls glow a dull orange, enough to read by. Tea grows wild in the cracks. People leave their kettles out on the rock and come back when they whistle, and socks dry on the walls in about four minutes. The only stove on the planet is in a museum, behind a rope.

Tea or coffee, if the floor boiled it for you?
