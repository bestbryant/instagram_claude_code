Sound is slow on Hush. A shout across the valley arrives four or five days later, quieter, usually after everyone has moved on. Arguments are carried out by letter, which tends to calm them down. In midwinter you can stand at the bottom of the valley and hear last summer, very faintly: dogs, a wedding, someone practising the trumpet badly.
