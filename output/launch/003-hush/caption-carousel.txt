Imaginary planet No. 003: Hush. A planet for people who reply to texts five days late.

Sound is slow on Hush. A shout across the valley arrives four or five days later, quieter, usually after everyone has moved on. Arguments are carried out by letter, which tends to calm them down. In midwinter you can stand at the bottom of the valley and hear last summer: dogs, a wedding, someone practising the trumpet badly. In March a man proposed across the valley. Her answer is due on Thursday.
