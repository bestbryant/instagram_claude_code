Mapping invented planets, two a day. Leave a name and I'll find yours.

Invented planet No. 043: Fetch. A planet for every dad who said no to the dog.

Three hands up. Not Dad's.

Fetch: 340,000 people, a 26-hour day, one moon, and a dog in almost every house, though never the dog the house picked. On Choosing Day the puppies are let loose in the square and each one chooses a person. Children practise looking chosen for weeks: sitting nicely, a biscuit in every pocket. The dogs go for whoever is pretending not to care. Most years, that's a dad.

I was chosen too, by an elderly terrier who sat on my map and wouldn't move. I've had to redraw the coast.

(On Earth, a border collie called Chaser learned the names of 1,022 toys. Most dads still call theirs 'the dog'.)

Who did your dog choose?
