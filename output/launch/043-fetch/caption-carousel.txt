Imaginary planet No. 043: Fetch. A planet for every dad who said no to the dog.

On Fetch, the dogs do the choosing. On Choosing Day the puppies are let loose in the square, and each one trots off and picks a person. Children practise looking chosen for weeks. Three children had asked for a dog on every birthday since they could talk, and every year their dad said no: the hair, the walks, the good chair. That year the smallest puppy walked straight past all three of them and sat on his foot. They nap in the good chair every afternoon. The children still want a dog.

Names welcome below. I find a few planets each evening.
