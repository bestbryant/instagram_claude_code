Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 088: Tint. A planet for couples who only have weekends.

One of these two is only home at weekends.

Tint: 4.4 million people, a 24-hour day, one moon, and colour that goes wherever the person you love goes. When they leave the house, it leaves with them: the garden, the telly, the tea. Most people get it back by teatime. Craig's wife works away from Monday to Friday, so Craig spends the week in black and white, and the neighbours know it's Friday when the hedges go green, one house at a time.

I nipped out for stamps. My landlady went beige.

(On Earth, your eyes stop seeing colour in dim light and hand over to cells that only see grey. It all comes back in the morning.)

Does your tea go grey when yours is away?
