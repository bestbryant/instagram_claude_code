Imaginary planet No. 088: Tint. A planet for couples who only have weekends.

On Tint, colour follows whoever you love. When they leave the house, it leaves with them: the garden goes grey, the telly goes grey, the tea goes the colour of dishwater. Most people get it back by teatime. Craig's wife works away, Monday to Friday, so Craig lives in black and white from Monday morning on. On Friday night, the street can tell when her car turns in, because the hedges go green one house at a time. Craig has been in odd socks since Monday.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
