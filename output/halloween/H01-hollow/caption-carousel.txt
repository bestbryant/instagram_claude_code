Hollow

The ghosts on Hollow are shy. They live in wardrobes and under the stairs, and if you say boo they faint, which is awkward for everyone. At the end of October the living go door to door to apologise for the year's frights. The ghosts leave sweets out on the step to say it's fine, really.
