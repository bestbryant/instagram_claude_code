Imaginary planet No. H03: Mothwing. A planet for people who sleep with the lamp on.

The moths on Mothwing have wingspans like kites, and they live on light. Each night they lift the moonlight off the fields and carry it back up. Leave a candle in the window and they'll take that too, but they leave a little glowing dust on the sill, which lasts until morning.
