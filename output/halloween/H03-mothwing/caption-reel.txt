Invented planet No. H03: Mothwing. A planet for people who pretend to be out on Halloween.

Mothwing has 19,000 people, two moons and a 36-hour day, so the nights run to eighteen hours and the moths are never short of work. They have wingspans like kites and live on light. Every night they lift the moonlight off the fields and carry it back up, and each one keeps the same round of windows all its life. A candle left on the sill after dark counts as a donation. They take the flame, leave the wick, and leave a little glowing dust that lasts until breakfast. Nobody sells nightlights. There'd be no point.

Nobody has ever been hurt by one. Several people have been made to feel terrible.

I left a torch out on my first night, to be helpful. I got it back. Switched off.

What does your family say they're not made of?
