Invented planet No. 082: Autumn. A planet for everyone who knew straight away.

Jake and Liam haven't met yet.

Autumn: 3.9 million people, a 26-hour day, one moon, and the leaves aren't the only thing that falls. People here physically fall in love. Catch the right eye and down you go, in public, usually holding shopping. Cafés have soft floors. Weddings have a nurse on standby. Jake had been getting the same train as Liam for three months before either of them looked up.

I was introduced to a very handsome fisherman. I'd like to say I stayed on my feet.

(The story goes that 'fall' for autumn comes from 'the fall of the leaf', back in the 1500s. On Autumn, the leaves are the least of it.)
