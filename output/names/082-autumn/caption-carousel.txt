Imaginary planet No. 082: Autumn. A planet for everyone who knew straight away.

On Autumn, the leaves aren't the only thing that falls. People here physically fall in love: catch the right eye and down you go, in public, usually with shopping. Cafés have soft floors, and weddings have a nurse on standby. Jake had been getting the same train as Liam for three months before either of them looked up. When he did, he went down in the aisle. Liam went to help, and went down too. It was mutual.
