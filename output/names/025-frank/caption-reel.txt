Mapping invented planets, two a day. Leave a name and I'll find yours.

Invented planet No. 025: Frank. A planet for people who can't fake a compliment.

Eleven thousand days without a fib. So far.

Frank: 60,000 people, a 25-hour day, no moons, and a board in every square counting the days since anyone told a white lie. Weather reports come with the forecaster's mood, and the estate agents have all retrained. For a big birthday, the card says 'Much older'. A compliment here is so rare that people have them framed, and Ada's father has three.

Visitors don't count on the board, which was lucky for me. I told my landlady I loved her wallpaper. She's given me a roll to take home.

(The story goes that April Fools' Day began when France moved New Year's Day to 1 January in the 1560s, and anyone still celebrating at the start of April got laughed at. Frank has never needed the day.)

Finish the bowl, or tell her?
