Imaginary planet No. 025: Frank. A planet for people who can't fake a compliment.

Frank prides itself on its honesty. Menus list which dishes the cook regrets, wedding speeches are short and alarming, and birthday cards just say 'Older.' A board in the square counts the days since anyone told a white lie. It had reached 11,042 the morning Ada made her father soup.
