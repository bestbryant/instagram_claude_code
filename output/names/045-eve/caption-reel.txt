Invented planet No. 045: Eve. A planet for every last-minute Christmas shopper.

Read the sign by the trees.

Eve: 2.4 million people, a 25-hour day, one moon, and Christmas, which has been tomorrow for nine hundred years. The stockings are up. The snow is down. The children are far too excited to sleep, and have been since birth. Every evening the whole planet goes to bed early so it'll come quicker. Nobody has ever bought a present, because why would you, with a whole day to go?

I brought one for my host. She cried, and then she panicked.

(The story goes that Christmas Eve is part of Christmas because the old church day began at sunset, so the feast started the evening before. On Eve, it never got past the evening.)

What would you buy with one morning's notice?
