Imaginary planet No. 045: Eve. A planet for every last-minute Christmas shopper.

On Eve, Christmas is always tomorrow. It has been tomorrow for nine hundred years. The stockings are up, the snow is down, and the children are far too excited to sleep, which is a problem, because they haven't slept since they were born. Nobody has ever needed to buy a present: why would you, with a whole day to go? Then, one morning, the sign in the square changed.
