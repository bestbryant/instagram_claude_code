Imaginary planet No. 083: Molly. A planet for every husband with man flu.

On Molly, colds come out as weather. A sniffle is a flurry, a cough is a hard frost, and anything with a temperature closes the airport. Molly had a cold last week and carried on as normal, in a light flurry. Then her husband Keith caught it. The roads closed. The schools closed. Molly made soup and carried it out to him through the drifts. He says he nearly died.
