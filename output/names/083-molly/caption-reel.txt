Any name you leave gets a planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Invented planet No. 083: Molly. A planet for every husband with man flu.

The man on the left is the forecast.

Molly: 2.8 million people, a 23-hour day, two moons, and colds that come out as weather. A sniffle is a flurry. A cough is a hard frost. Anything with a temperature closes the airport, and the forecast is mostly a list of who's poorly. Molly had a cold last week and carried on as normal, in a light flurry. Then her husband caught it.

I had a sniffle on my first morning. The weather service named it after me.

(On Earth, a Canadian doctor reviewed the evidence for 'man flu' in the BMJ in 2017 and concluded men may really suffer a bit more. Keith has printed it out.)

Is yours a flurry or a blizzard?
