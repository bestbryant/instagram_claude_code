Invented planet No. 050: Ray. A planet for people who know they're Mum's favourite.

The dog's been in every shot.

Ray: 5.1 million people, a 28-hour day, no moons, and a sun with favourites. Every family here has its own small sun, and it shines on whoever Mum loves best, which she has always said is nonsense. Siblings follow it the way other planets follow the football. Family photos are studied like evidence. Jen has kept a log since primary school. Tom rings Mum every day and says it isn't tactics.

I stayed one night with a family of five. Their sun came and sat on me. I haven't been asked back.

(On Earth, sunlight takes about eight minutes and twenty seconds to reach you, so it's always shining on where you were. On Ray, it knows exactly where you are.)
