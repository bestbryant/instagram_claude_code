Imaginary planet No. 050: Ray. A planet for people who know they're Mum's favourite.

On Ray, every family has its own small sun, and it shines on Mum's favourite. Mum says this is nonsense. Jen, Tom and Lily have argued about it for years: it moves about, and nobody's ever sure. At Mum's birthday, all three came home and stood very still in the garden. It picked the dog. Nobody's talking to the dog.
