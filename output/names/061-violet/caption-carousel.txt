Imaginary planet No. 061: Violet. A planet for whoever sat with the new kid.

On Violet, nobody can see a newcomer until someone sits down next to them. Then they're simply there, as if they always had been. Every school keeps a bench by the gate for it. Zara started in the spring term and spent her whole first week invisible: lunch on her own, the register unanswered. On Friday, Josh sat down beside her to tie his shoe. She appeared mid-sandwich. Twenty years later, she was his best man.
