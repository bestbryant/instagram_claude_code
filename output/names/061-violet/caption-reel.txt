Mapping invented planets, two a day. Leave a name and I'll find yours.

Invented planet No. 061: Violet. A planet for whoever sat with the new kid.

Mind the gap in the middle of that row.

Violet: 2.6 million people, a 23-hour day, no moons, and nobody can see a newcomer until someone sits down beside them. Then they're simply there, as if they always had been. Every school keeps a bench by the gate for it, and most first days have a quiet hour. Zara's lasted a week. Josh only sat down to tie his shoe.

I was invisible for my first two days here. A dog found me. We're still in touch.

(The story goes that the 'buddy bench', where a lonely child sits so that someone will come and ask them to play, reached American playgrounds in 2013 through a second-grader in Pennsylvania who'd seen one in a brochure from Germany. On Violet, the bench does the rest.)

When were you last the new one?
