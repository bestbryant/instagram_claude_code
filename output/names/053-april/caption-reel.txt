Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 053: April. A planet for every grandad who says it's hay fever.

That cloud is mostly the bride's mum.

April: 3.6 million people, a 24-hour day, one moon, and the wettest weddings in the known universe. Happy tears fall as rain, so the forecast is mostly births, reunions and the last episodes of things. Brides book a marquee. Grandad has never needed one. He has stood bone dry through four weddings, two christenings and a cup final, hands in his pockets.

I cried at a sunset on my second night. The campsite has asked me not to come back.

(The story goes that in Italy they say 'sposa bagnata, sposa fortunata': a wet bride is a lucky bride. On April, it just means her dad came.)

Rain or shine, at the last wedding you went to?
