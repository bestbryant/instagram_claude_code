Imaginary planet No. 053: April. A planet for every grandad who says it's hay fever.

On April, happy tears fall as rain. Weddings are very wet, and graduations need wellies. Grandad has never cried: not at weddings, not even at the football. He has never owned an umbrella. Then his granddaughter said her first word, and the word was 'Grandad'. It's still raining, and there's a lake now. 'Hay fever,' he says.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
