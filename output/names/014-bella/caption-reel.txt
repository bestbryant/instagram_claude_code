Invented planet No. 014: Bella. Every Bella gets this planet.

The tax office would like a word.

Bella is low blue hills, one long view of the sea, and the Beauty Act. It passed in a hurry, the morning after the old mayor called his own wedding 'fine'. Saying 'fine' now costs three compliments, paid in person, and 'not bad' is under review. The tax office has a perfect five-star rating, because leaving fewer stars is not beautiful. Teo was fined for insulting a public building by comparison. Nina had the fine framed. I called the view 'fine' on my first morning. I still owe one compliment.

What would your sign say?

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
