Imaginary planet No. 014: Bella. Every Bella gets this planet.

On Bella, everything has to be described as beautiful, by law. The weather report says beautiful rain. The dentist is beautiful. When the new tax office opened, it was declared 'genuinely beautiful' by a man in a hat who had clearly been told to. The people of Bella are very relaxed about all of this, which is, they would want me to say, beautiful.

Know a Bella? Send her this. It's beautiful.
