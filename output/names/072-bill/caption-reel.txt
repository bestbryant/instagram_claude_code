Invented planet No. 072: Bill. A planet for the friend who always pays.

The sun's out. Someone's about to pay.

Bill: 6.4 million people, a day that depends on who's buying, no moons, and everything paid for with daylight. A coffee costs a few minutes of afternoon. A round of drinks costs the evening. Careful people have long, golden summers; the generous live mostly by lamplight. Dan has paid for everyone since school: every round, every taxi, every birthday meal. It's been night at his house for years. He reads by the fridge light and doesn't mind.

I tipped well at dinner. I had breakfast by torchlight.

(On Earth, England taxed windows from 1696 to 1851, and people bricked theirs up to pay less. The story goes that's where 'daylight robbery' comes from. On Bill, it's just called paying.)

Who in your lot would be living in the dark?
