Imaginary planet No. 072: Bill. A planet for the friend who always pays.

On Bill, you pay with daylight. A coffee costs a few minutes of afternoon; a round of drinks costs the evening. Careful people have long, golden summers, and the generous live mostly by lamplight. Dan has paid for everyone since school: every round, every taxi, every birthday meal. It's been night at his house for years. He reads by the fridge light and doesn't mind. So his friends paid him back, all of it, at once. The sun came up over his house so hard the neighbours went out for sunglasses. He got the drinks in.
