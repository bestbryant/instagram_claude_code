Invented planet No. 032: Norman. A planet for the one everyone else steers by.

Read the signpost on the left.

Norman is a bright, blowy planet of 300,000 people and one fixed point: a retired joiner called Norman, who is north. Maps are drawn from his kitchen. Captains ring ahead before he goes to the shops. When he had his knee done, two ferries and a flock of geese turned up at the hospital. So he stayed put, for everyone, and wrote to Pat every week. She lives in the far south, which until this year was the bit of the map nobody visited.

I tried to walk north to meet him and spent an afternoon following him round a garden centre.

(Earth's north wanders too. Its magnetic pole has drifted from Canada towards Siberia, at more than fifty kilometres a year at its fastest. It has never once gone to see anyone.)

How far would you move for someone?
