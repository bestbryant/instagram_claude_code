Imaginary planet No. 032: Norman. A planet for the one everyone else steers by.

On Norman, north is a man, also called Norman. Every compass points at him, and when he nips out for milk, so does the navy. For thirty-one years he kept still, for everyone's sake, and wrote to Pat in the far south. This year, he went. Every compass on the planet swung round to Pat's, and the navy has been very understanding.

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
