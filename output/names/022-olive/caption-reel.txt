A planet for people who can't go to bed on an argument. (Imaginary planet No. 022: Olive)

The sun behind those two is on overtime.

Olive is a warm planet of 7 million people, terraced hills and very long evenings. The sun goes down when the last argument on the planet has been made up, and not a minute before, so it usually sets around nine, give or take a sulk. The papers print the time every morning, with names. Children learn 'sorry' before 'it was him'.

I fell out with my own map on my first evening. Seven million people waited up until I apologised to it.

On Earth, 'do not let the sun go down on your anger' comes from Paul's letter to the Ephesians. On Olive, the sun enforces it.

How long would your house keep the sun up?

Got a name? Put it below and I'll find the planet it came from.
