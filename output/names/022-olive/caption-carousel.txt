Imaginary planet No. 022: Olive. A planet for people who can't go to bed on an argument.

On Olive, you can't go to bed angry: the sun won't go down until every argument on the planet is made up. Most evenings that's quick. Election night lasts a month. When Ruth and Dev had words about the dishwasher, the sun stayed up for three days, the neighbours came round, and then the Prime Minister. Then Ruth made him a tea, and it set before he'd had a sip.
