Invented planet No. 094: Gary. A planet for every couple arguing over baby names.

Leave a name: it gets its own planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

The one on his shoulders gets the final say.

Gary: 5.6 million people, a 27-hour day, one moon, and babies who pick their own names. Parents can argue for nine months if they like. The day it arrives, the baby decides, and a board goes up outside the house so the street knows. Ryan wanted Arthur. Megan wanted Theo. The baby went with Gary.

A friend there let me hold her newborn for a minute. It named itself after me. I haven't been asked back.

(The story goes that the actor Frank Cooper only became Gary because his agent came from Gary, Indiana. Nobody asked Frank either.)

How long did you two argue over names?
