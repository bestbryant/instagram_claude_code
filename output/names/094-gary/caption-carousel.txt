Imaginary planet No. 094: Gary. A planet for every couple arguing over baby names.

On Gary, babies pick their own names. Parents can argue for nine months if they like; the day it arrives, the baby decides, and a board goes up outside the house so the street knows. Ryan wanted Arthur. Megan wanted Theo. They had a list, a shortlist and a fortnight of not speaking. The baby went with Gary. Megan's ex is called Gary. Ryan calls him 'the baby'.
