Imaginary planet No. 047: Pete. A planet for people who say 'don't make a fuss'.

Everything on Pete is done for Pete's sake. The moons rise for Pete's sake, the northern lights go on for Pete's sake, and the daisies open and the band plays on the hill, all for Pete's sake. Pete is a quiet man who likes a crossword and hates a fuss. On his birthday he asked, very politely, if everyone could please stop. They stopped, for Pete's sake. The daisies shut and the moons went in. He meant a small fuss.
