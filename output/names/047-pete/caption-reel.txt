Mapping invented planets, two a day. Leave a name and I'll find yours.

Invented planet No. 047: Pete. A planet for people who say 'don't make a fuss'.

Only one person here isn't celebrating.

Pete: 93,000 people, a 24-hour day, three moons, and a planet that does everything for Pete's sake. The moons rise for Pete's sake. The northern lights go on for Pete's sake. The daisies open and the bakers bake and the brass band practises on the hill, all for Pete's sake, and Pete would really rather they didn't. He's a quiet man. He likes a crossword. On his birthday he asked, very politely, if everyone could please stop.

I told him I'd come all this way for his sake. He went the colour of a moon.

(The story goes that 'for Pete's sake' began as a polite way round 'for God's sake', with Saint Peter standing in. This Pete has never been made a saint, though people keep suggesting it.)

When did you last make a fuss of someone?
