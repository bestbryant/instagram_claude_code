Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 062: Ash. A planet for brothers who only trade insults.

Mick, on the left, has just been nice.

Ash: 1.9 million people, a 26-hour day, no moons, and so much static in the air that a kind word throws sparks. A small one crackles. A proper one, the kind you say a handful of times in a life, sets the hillside alight and brings out the fire brigade. So people here talk about the weather. Mick and Tony are brothers, and have managed a whole life on football and insults. Then Tony became a dad.

I told the hotel receptionist I loved the view. They evacuated the lobby.

(On Earth, a static shock you can feel takes about three thousand volts, and one you can see takes several thousand more. The meters on Ash melt before anyone can measure a proper 'I'm proud of you'.)

What's the kindest thing you've never said?
