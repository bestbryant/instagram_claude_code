Imaginary planet No. 062: Ash. A planet for brothers who only trade insults.

The air on Ash is full of static, and kind words set it off. A small one crackles. A proper one, the kind people only say a handful of times in a life, sets the whole hillside alight. So people here keep it light: the weather, the football, a dig about your haircut. Mick and Tony are brothers and have got by on insults since the playground. Then Tony became a dad, and Mick, in the hospital car park, said, 'You'll be brilliant.' The fire brigade said it was the best one they'd seen all year. Mick lost an eyebrow. Worth it.

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
