Invented planet No. 085: Dawn. A planet for people who fall asleep on you.

Someone at that stop did a double shift.

Dawn: 2.3 million people, a 27-hour day, no moons, and a rule about sleeping on people. Fall asleep on someone's shoulder and it counts as a proposal; if they let you stay there, that's a yes. Night buses are tense. Commuters sit bolt upright and drink coffee like it's their job. Laura had done a double shift, sat down next to a stranger at the bus stop, and was asleep on him before the timetable had finished lying.

I dozed off on a ferry. I'm engaged to a rucksack.

(The story goes that in Japan, sleeping in public is ordinary enough to have its own word: inemuri. Nobody gets engaged.)
