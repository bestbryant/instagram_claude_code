Imaginary planet No. 085: Dawn. A planet for people who fall asleep on you.

On Dawn, falling asleep on someone counts as a proposal, and staying still counts as a yes. Night buses are very tense. Commuters drink a great deal of coffee, and everybody sits bolt upright, like they're at the dentist. Laura had done a double shift. At the bus stop she sat down next to a stranger, and was asleep on his shoulder before the timetable had finished lying. He could have coughed, or shrugged, or stood up. He didn't wake her. He let the last bus go. They've been married for years, and she still does it.
