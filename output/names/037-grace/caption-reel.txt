A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 037: Grace. A planet for people who aren't ready for the last dance.

Look who's still dancing at breakfast.

Grace is 1.3 million people in soft, low hills, with gravity light enough that nobody's feet ever get tired. The rule is older than anyone can remember: the band plays until the last dancer sits down. Musicians retire young. Wedding invitations give a start time and a polite estimate.

On my first night I was the last one on the floor, looking for my shoe. The band played until I found it.

(On Earth, the story goes, the first dance at a wedding comes from the old formal balls, where the guests of honour opened the floor. On Grace, it's the last dance everyone remembers.)

Which song would keep you on your feet?
