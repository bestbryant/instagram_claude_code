Imaginary planet No. 037: Grace. A planet for people who aren't ready for the last dance.

On Grace, the band never stops first: it plays until everyone sits down. Weddings run for weeks, and drummers retire mid-song. At Rosie's wedding, the dancing started at eight. By midnight, four were left. By breakfast, two: Rosie and her dad. The groom went to bed at ten. Her dad isn't ready to sit down yet. Nor is she.
