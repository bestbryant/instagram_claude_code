Invented planet No. 049: Stella. A planet for every sister who talked after lights-out.

The two on the hill grew up in that house.

Stella: 1.4 million people, a 26-hour day, no moons, and a night sky that is mostly children. Anything whispered after lights-out goes up as a star, so the busiest skies are over bunk beds, sleepovers and sisters, and about a third of any family's sky is a parent saying 'Go to sleep.' Joan and Meg's is the famous one: sixteen years of one bedroom, and a long row about whose turn it was to get the light.

I said goodnight to my map after lights-out. It's up there now, small and faint, near the edge.

(On Earth, the light from Vega set off about twenty-five years ago, so a whisper from your childhood would only now be arriving. On Stella, it's up before you've finished it.)

What did you whisper about after lights-out?
