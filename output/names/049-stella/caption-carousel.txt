Imaginary planet No. 049: Stella. A planet for every sister who talked after lights-out.

On Stella, anything whispered after lights-out turns into a star. The sky is mostly children: bunk beds, sleepovers, and a great many parents saying 'Go to sleep.' Joan and her sister Meg shared a room for sixteen years, and the sky over their house was the brightest in town. They're both nans now, and live miles apart. Every night, Joan rings Meg and whispers, 'You awake?' Meg always is.
