Imaginary planet No. 098: June. A planet for everyone with a sister and one bathroom.

On June, showers last a season. The water's lovely; it's just that the year carries on outside without you. June went in on the first of June. Her brother Rob waited at the door with his towel. The daisies went, then the leaves, then the snow came, and Rob sat down. She came out on Christmas Eve, pink and very relaxed. Rob had grown a beard. She'd used all the hot water.
