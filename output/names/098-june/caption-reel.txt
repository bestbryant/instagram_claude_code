Rob's waiting for the bathroom.

June: 640,000 people, a 25-hour day, three moons, and showers that last a season. Step in, and the year carries on outside without you. A quick rinse takes a week. A long soak, you miss a birthday. Families book the bathroom like a holiday, and whoever's second always loses.

I had one shower there, a quick one. I came out to find my hire car had been towed in the spring.

(The story goes that the month of June is named after Juno, the Roman goddess of marriage. On June, it's named after the bathroom.)

How long would you wait outside the door?
