Imaginary planet No. 087: Hilary. A planet for couples who dance in the kitchen.

On Hilary, dancing raises the roof, and the house, and the ground underneath it. One good night out leaves a bump outside the chip shop. Ballrooms are built in valleys and steadily stop being valleys. Ravi and Mark have danced in the kitchen after tea every night for decades, badly, to whatever's on the radio, including the shipping forecast. Their house used to sit at the bottom of a gentle slope. It's now on top of a hill, and the view is incredible. The postman's never been fitter.
