Invented planet No. 087: Hilary. A planet for couples who dance in the kitchen.

That house used to be at the bottom.

Hilary: 3.2 million people, a 25-hour day, two moons, and dancing that raises the roof, along with the house and the ground underneath it. One good night out leaves a bump outside the chip shop. Ballrooms are built at the bottom of valleys and, steadily, stop being valleys. Ravi and Mark dance in the kitchen every night after tea, badly, to whatever's on, including the shipping forecast. They have done for decades.

I did a little shuffle at a wedding. There's a speed bump outside the church now.

(On Earth, fans dancing at a stadium concert in Seattle in 2023 shook the ground as hard as a magnitude 2.3 earthquake, according to the local seismologists. The ground stayed exactly where it was.)

Has your kitchen gone up in the world?
