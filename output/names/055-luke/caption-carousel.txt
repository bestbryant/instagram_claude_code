Imaginary planet No. 055: Luke. A planet for flatmates who fight over the heating.

On Luke, the sky's thermostat is in Priya's hallway. Priya likes it tropical. Her flatmate Ellie likes it snowing, and says so by walking past the dial very slowly. Most days this winter the neighbours have had four seasons before breakfast, and they keep a parasol and a shovel by the door. After a long talk and a short row, the flat agreed a compromise: lukewarm drizzle. Nobody is happy. Everybody is used to it.
