A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 055: Luke. A planet for flatmates who fight over the heating.

The dial is just inside that front door.

Luke: 390,000 people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and one thermostat, in the hallway of a rented flat. Priya likes it tropical. Ellie likes it snowing. Neither has read the tenancy agreement, which is very clear that the weather is shared. The neighbours keep a parasol and a shovel by the door and no longer ask.

I was there for one morning. I got sunburnt and snowed on before lunch.

(On Earth, a 2015 study in Nature Climate Change found that the standard formula for office temperatures was worked out for the metabolism of a forty-year-old man. On Luke, it's worked out by whoever touched the dial last.)

Who in your house is in charge of the dial?
