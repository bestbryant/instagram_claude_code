Imaginary planet No. 063: Kate. A planet for every friend who's 'nearly there'.

There's a town on Kate called Five Minutes Away. It's about an hour from everywhere. Everyone who's running late is from there: the friend who's just parking, the one who's literally just leaving now, the one who's nearly there. The buses leave in five minutes, always. Its twin town is On My Way. Kate moved there at school and never quite left. Naturally, the town elected her mayor. Her swearing-in started an hour ago.
