Invented planet No. 063: Kate. A planet for every friend who's 'nearly there'.

Everyone in that crowd is running late.

Kate: 3.3 million people, a 24-hour day (give or take), no moons, and a town called Five Minutes Away, which is about an hour from everywhere. Everyone who's running late is from there: the friend who's just parking, the one who's literally leaving now, the one who's nearly there. The buses leave in five minutes, always. Kate moved there at school and never quite left.

I told them I was five minutes away. They gave me a house.

(The story goes that Swiss railways count a train as late once it's three minutes behind. On Kate, three minutes behind is considered early.)

How far away is your five minutes?
