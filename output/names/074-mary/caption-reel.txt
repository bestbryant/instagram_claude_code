Invented planet No. 074: Mary. A planet for the one who always has plasters.

Mary packed light today.

Mary: 3.7 million people, a 25-hour day, one moon and a spare. Every group of friends here has a Mary. Her bag has plasters, tissues, a sewing kit, a charger for every phone ever made and, on the night of Beth's picnic, a moon. Nobody asks how. The bag has never been weighed, and she'd rather you didn't.

I asked her for a pen. She asked what colour.

(On Earth, the Moon is about 3,474 kilometres across. Mary says it folds.)

What's the strangest thing anyone's ever found in their bag for you?
