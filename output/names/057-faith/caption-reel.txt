Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 057: Faith. A planet for the teacher who believed in you.

Kieran's village had never seen that sunset.

Faith: 870,000 people, a 26-hour day, one moon, and a geography that depends on who believes in whom. Most days it's small stuff: a hill shuffles over for a nervous driver. The big one stood between Kieran's village and everywhere else, and nobody, including the mountain, thought he'd get past it. Miss Hart did. The maps were redrawn by the weekend.

(On Earth, mountains move too: India is still pushing into Asia at four or five centimetres a year, so the Himalayas are, strictly speaking, on their way somewhere.)
