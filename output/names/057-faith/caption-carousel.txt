Imaginary planet No. 057: Faith. A planet for the teacher who believed in you.

On Faith, believing in someone moves mountains. Most of the time it's small stuff: a hill shuffles over for a nervous driver, a ridge lies down for a cyclist. Kieran's village sits behind the biggest mountain on the planet, and nobody thought he'd get to university, least of all the mountain. Then his teacher, Miss Hart, believed in him. The mountain moved out of the way overnight. The bus takes ten minutes now. Miss Hart is already on the next one.
