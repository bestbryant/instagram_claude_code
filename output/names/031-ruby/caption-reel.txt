Invented planet No. 031: Ruby. A planet for everyone who's said 'you too' to a waiter.

Somebody is to blame for that sky.

Ruby is a mild, blue planet of twelve million people with nowhere to hide. The sky goes pink whenever anyone on it is embarrassed, and red when it's bad, so the whole planet knows by lunchtime. A trip on the stairs is a rosy afternoon. A reply-all is a week of sunsets. Poker never caught on, and the weather forecast is basically gossip. The evening newsreader had gone twenty years without a single blush.

I waved back at a man who was waving at someone behind me. The whole planet knew by teatime.

(On Earth, a sunset is red because low light crosses about thirty-eight times as much air as light from straight overhead, and the blue gets scattered out on the way. On Ruby, it's because somebody's dad is dancing.)

What turned your sky red this week?

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
