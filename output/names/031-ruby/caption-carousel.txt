Imaginary planet No. 031: Ruby. A planet for everyone who's said 'you too' to a waiter.

On Ruby, the sky blushes. Whenever anyone on the planet is embarrassed it goes a little pink, so most sunsets are someone saying 'you too' to a waiter, and wedding speeches are given after dark. Nobody had ever seen it properly red. Then, live, to eleven million people, the evening newsreader signed off with 'Love you, bye.' Four million said it back. It stayed red for a week.
