Imaginary planet No. 075: Bob. A planet for the neighbour who'll have a look.

On Bob, every street has a neighbour who can fix anything: the tap, the boiler, the bike, the wobbly table in the café. So the day the sun broke, everyone rang Bob next door. He put his coat on, said 'Right,' and went to have a look. It was working again before his tea went cold, slightly to the left. There's one left over. He says it was like that when he got there.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
