Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 075: Bob. A planet for the neighbour who'll have a look.

Bob hasn't had a look at the sun yet.

Bob: 910,000 people, a 24-hour day, and suns, plural, since Bob had a look. Every street here has one neighbour who can fix things, and everyone rings him. When the sun broke, Bob put his coat on, said 'Right,' and went up with a torch. Nobody saw what he did. It was working again before his tea went cold. It hums a bit now.

I asked whether he'd read the instructions. He laughed for quite a long time.

(On Earth, the Sun is about 109 Earths across, which is a lot of sun for one man and a torch.)

What did your neighbour fix that was never quite the same again?
