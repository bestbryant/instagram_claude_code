Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 044: Joy. A planet for every grandad with one good joke.

Watch the tall one in the middle.

Joy: 1.2 million people, a 22-hour day, two moons, and gravity so gentle that a good laugh lifts you clean off the ground. Comedians play to empty rooms and look up for the applause. Funerals are held indoors, under low ceilings. Sunday lunch is the one safe meal in most families, because somebody's grandad does the jokes, and the jokes are terrible. Then, one Sunday, between the roast and the crumble, he told a good one.

I laughed at a sign in a café window and spent the afternoon on the roof.

(On Earth, the psychologist Robert Provine found we're about thirty times more likely to laugh with other people than alone. Grandad had a full table.)

What's the one good joke in your family?
