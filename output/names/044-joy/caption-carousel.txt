Imaginary planet No. 044: Joy. A planet for every grandad with one good joke.

Gravity on Joy is gentle, and laughing makes you lighter, so a good laugh lifts you clean off the ground. Comedians play to empty rooms and look up for the applause. Funerals are held indoors. Sunday lunch, though, stays firmly on the ground in most families, because Grandad does the jokes, and Grandad's jokes are terrible. Then, one Sunday, between the roast and the crumble, he told a good one. The whole table went up. Grandad stayed down: he'd heard it before. It was the proudest day of his life.
