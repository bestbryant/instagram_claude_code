Invented planet No. 058: Melody. A planet for every brother singing in the shower.

One person on that hill started it.

Melody: 3.4 million people, a 25-hour day, two moons, and songs that spread like colds. Hum on the bus and the bus goes home humming. Shops aren't allowed to play music. Callum sang one line in the shower one morning, and by teatime the whole planet had it.

I caught it at passport control. I'm still humming it.

(On Earth, a Finnish study found that about nine in ten of the people it surveyed got a song stuck in their head at least once a week, usually just a fragment. On Melody, it's everyone's fragment.)

Have you ever caught a song off a stranger?
