Imaginary planet No. 058: Melody. A planet for every brother singing in the shower.

On Melody, catchy songs are actually catching. Hum something on the bus and the whole bus goes home humming it. Callum sang one line of a song in the shower one morning. His sister caught it through the bathroom door, the postman caught it from her, and by teatime the whole planet was singing it, in every kitchen and every queue. Callum doesn't know the second line. Neither does anyone else.
