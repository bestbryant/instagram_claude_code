Imaginary planet No. 024: Willow. A planet for people who save the good plates for best.

Willow has one tree. Not one kind: one tree. Everyone gets one afternoon under it in their life, as long as they like, and the queue is four hundred years long. Bett had hers on her wedding day. Sol had his the morning his daughter was born. Last spring it was Gaun's turn, and he's still standing next to it, saving it for something special. His family say he's the same with the good plates.

Drop a name below and I'll chart where it lives.
