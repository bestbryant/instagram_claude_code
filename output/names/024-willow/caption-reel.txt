A planet for people who save the good plates for best. (Imaginary planet No. 024: Willow)

The man nearest the tree is waiting.

Willow is a lake, 146,000 people and one tree, and every one of them is in the queue for it. When your turn comes you get one afternoon in its shade, as long as you like, and you can't swap it or sell it. Most people pick a wedding, a new baby or a very good book. Gaun's turn came up last spring. His family have stopped asking.

I leaned on it on my first morning. Four hundred years of queue cleared its throat.

In Japan, offices send their newest recruit out early in cherry-blossom season to hold a spot under the trees for that evening's party. It's called basho-tori. On Willow, the whole planet is holding one spot.

What would you save your afternoon for?
