Imaginary planet No. 012: Luna. Every Luna gets this planet.

Luna is the only planet in the atlas without a moon of its own, and it takes this very personally. Every spring there is a fund-raiser. So far they have raised enough for one large lamp on a tall pole, which is switched on and pointed at the sky for birthdays, anniversaries and anyone having a bad week.

Know a Luna? This one's theirs.
