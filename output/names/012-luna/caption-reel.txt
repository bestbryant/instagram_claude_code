Every Luna gets this planet. (Imaginary planet No. 012: Luna)

Watch the sign by the lamp.

Luna is named after a moon it has never had. In the autumn of 1987 one came very close, slowed down, and kept going. Nobody says its name. It knows what it did.

The Moon Fund has met every spring since: bake sales, a sponsored silence, one raffle that got out of hand. It stands at 3%. Meanwhile there's the lamp, on a forty-metre pole on the hill above town. When it comes on, the whole town goes 'ooh'. Every time. Mr Okafor keeps the waiting list in a ring binder at the post office. Bad weeks are on the honour system. The night it's booked for Luna is the anniversary. Nobody mentions that either.

Local rule: do not mention other planets' moons in front of Luna. Especially Luma's.

Every Luna is on the list. For life.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
