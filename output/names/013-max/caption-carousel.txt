Imaginary planet No. 013: Max. Every Max gets this planet.

Everything on Max is set to maximum. The rivers run at full volume, the sun is on its brightest setting, and the birds know one song, which is loud. There used to be a quiet setting. It was removed by public vote, and the vote was also very loud. The library is now the noisiest building on the planet, because it had to compete.
