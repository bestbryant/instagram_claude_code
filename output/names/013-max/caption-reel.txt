Every Max gets this planet. (Imaginary planet No. 013: Max)

Someone between the megaphones isn't shouting.

The sun is on its brightest setting, and so are the birds. The rivers run at full volume. Bedtime is set to maximum too, and nobody has reached it yet.

Nell got the planet's only whispering licence at seven, because she was the only child on Max who had never been told to keep it down. She grew up to be the librarian and kept it in a drawer at the issue desk, unspent, for seventy-nine years. The quiet didn't last. The shushing has.

I applied for a licence while I was there. Turned down: nobody heard me apply.

What would you spend one whisper on?

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
