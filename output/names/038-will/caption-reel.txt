Invented planet No. 038: Will. A planet for people who are up at five because the dog is.

That board is counting votes.

Will is 6.2 million people, rolling hills and a sun that only comes up if enough of them want it to. Most mornings it's a formality: the kettles go on, everyone stands at a window and wants it, and the count goes up on a board in every square. The very old are excused. They say they've wanted enough sunrises for one lifetime.

I slept through my first morning. The street sent round a doctor.

(On Earth, the air bends sunlight over the horizon, so you see the sun come up about two minutes before it really has. On Will, it waits to be asked.)

What time would you set the sun for?

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
