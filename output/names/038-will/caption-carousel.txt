Imaginary planet No. 038: Will. A planet for people who are up at five because the dog is.

On Will, the sun only rises if enough people want it to, so every morning the whole planet gets up early and wants it. Teenagers are a national problem. School mornings are always a bit late. After New Year's Eve, the whole planet slept in, and the sky stayed dark. Then, at five, the sun rose anyway. One spaniel had wanted it very much.
