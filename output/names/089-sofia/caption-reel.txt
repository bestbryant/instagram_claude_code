Invented planet No. 089: Sofia. A planet for couples who sit closer every year.

Neither armchair is where it started.

Sofia: 1.7 million people, a 26-hour day, no moons, and armchairs that inch towards whoever their owner loves, about a foot a year. Newlyweds' chairs start at opposite ends of the lounge, and you can tell how long a couple's been married with a tape measure. Brenda and Ken's started either side of the fireplace, a very long time ago.

I stayed a week in a hotel there. By the last night, my armchair was up against the minibar.

(The story goes that the Victorians made an S-shaped 'courting chair', so a couple could sit face to face without quite touching. On Sofia, give it a silver wedding.)

Which of you would do most of the inching?
