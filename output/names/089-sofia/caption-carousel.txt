Imaginary planet No. 089: Sofia. A planet for couples who sit closer every year.

On Sofia, armchairs inch towards whoever their owner loves, about a foot a year. Newlyweds' chairs start at opposite ends of the lounge, and you can tell how long a couple's been married with a tape measure. Brenda and Ken's chairs started either side of the fireplace. Every year they shuffled a little closer, mostly while they were asleep in them. By their silver wedding, they touched, right in front of the screen. Nobody can get to the telly.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
