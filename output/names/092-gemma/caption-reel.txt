Any name you leave gets a planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

Invented planet No. 092: Gemma. A planet for every twin who comes as a set.

Those two aren't getting very far.

Gemma: 1.4 million people, a 20-hour day, two moons, and twins who can't get more than ten feet apart. Walk off in a huff and you snap straight back, shoulder to shoulder, wherever you were going. Twins share a bedroom, a desk and a bus seat for life. Gemma moved to Leeds. Jade came too. Then Gemma got married, and the bouquet only had one place to go.

I waited at a bus stop there behind a pair of twins. One wanted the number nine, the other wanted the twelve. They're still at the bus stop.

(The story goes that Twinsburg, Ohio, is named after twin brothers who married sisters, died on the same day and share a grave. On Gemma, that's just good planning.)

Could you share a bus seat for life?
