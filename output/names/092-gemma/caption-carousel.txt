Imaginary planet No. 092: Gemma. A planet for every twin who comes as a set.

On Gemma, twins can't get more than ten feet apart. Walk off in a huff and you snap straight back, shoulder to shoulder, wherever you were going. Twins here share a bedroom, a desk and a bus seat for life, and most of their opinions. Gemma moved to Leeds for work. Jade came too. Then Gemma got married, with her sister pressed against one arm and her husband against the other, and when she threw the bouquet there was only one person in range. Jade caught it. Obviously. The honeymoon was very cosy.

Names welcome below. I find a few planets each evening.
