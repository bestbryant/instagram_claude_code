Imaginary planet No. 016: Milo. Every Milo gets this planet.

The soil on Milo is malted chocolate powder, three metres deep. Farming is mostly stirring. When the warm rain comes, the ground goes thick and glossy and the whole planet smells like the bottom of a school mug. Everyone on Milo is cheerful and very slightly over-energetic. A few of them eat the ground dry, by the spoonful, which the others consider showing off.
