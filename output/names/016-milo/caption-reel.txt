Invented planet No. 016: Milo. Every Milo gets this planet.

Look again at the mountains.

The soil on Milo is malted chocolate powder, three metres deep, and the rain falls warm. Everyone here is cheerful and very slightly over-energetic. Morale is excellent. The tallest lump dates from a very good party in 1911. Dunking biscuits in the river is against the law, and a few grown-ups still eat the field dry, by the spoonful, which the others consider showing off.

I went out with the children at dawn. I found four.

On Earth, malt powder lumps the same way: the outside of a clump turns sticky and seals the dry powder in, which is why the lumps crunch.

Lumps: the best bit, or bad stirring?
