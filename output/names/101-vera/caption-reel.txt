Invented planet No. 101: Vera. A planet for the neighbour who takes in your parcels.

Vera is always in.

Vera: 940,000 people, a 30-hour day, no moons, and a lighthouse on every street for whoever's nosiest. Nobody votes. It just goes up one night beside the right house, and the beam does the rest. Ships could use them, in theory. Mostly they're for knowing who's had a delivery.

I stayed one night on Vera's street. By breakfast she knew where I was going next, and had views.

(The story goes that net curtains caught on in Victorian Britain so you could see out without being seen. Vera has never seen the point.)
