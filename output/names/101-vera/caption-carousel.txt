Imaginary planet No. 101: Vera. A planet for the neighbour who takes in your parcels.

On Vera, the nosiest neighbour on every street gets a lighthouse. Nobody votes. It just goes up one night beside the right house, and the beam does the rest. Vera's went up over her cottage on the cliff. She knew about the extension at number nine before the builders did. Then Vera went on a cruise, and the lighthouse went with her. For a fortnight, nobody on the street knew anything. When she got back, there'd been two affairs and a conservatory.

Drop a name below and I'll chart where it lives.
