Imaginary planet No. 017: Daisy. Every Daisy gets this planet.

The fields on Daisy are covered in daisies, and every one of them knows whether he loves you or not. People stopped pulling petals years ago and just ask the flowers directly. On a quiet evening the meadow sounds like very light applause, with the occasional boo. First dates are held in car parks, well away from any flowers.
