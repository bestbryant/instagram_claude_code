Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 017: Daisy. Every Daisy gets this planet.

Second time through, look at where Bert and May are standing.

Daisy is mostly meadow, and the daisies are never wrong. They answer out loud: light applause, or some booing. They may not be asked about third parties, which is why florists here need a licence and a good lawyer. The car park was built after one very loud first date. Like ours, they shut at dusk (the name comes from 'day's eye'), and it takes a lot to open them again. There's one path across the middle of the meadow now. It's theirs.

I tried asking about someone back home. Third parties, apparently.

Would you ask the flowers?
