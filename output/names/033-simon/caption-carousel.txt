Imaginary planet No. 033: Simon. A planet for the parents of very small bosses.

On Simon, everyone does what Simon says, and Simon is quite small. So the whole planet hops to work, eats pudding first and, as of this afternoon, holds a fish. At bedtime he yawned and told everyone to go to bed. Nobody moved: he hadn't said 'Simon says'. Then he fell asleep on his dad's shoulders, and nobody's waking him.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
