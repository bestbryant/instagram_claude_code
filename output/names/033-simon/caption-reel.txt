A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 033: Simon. A planet for the parents of very small bosses.

Count the fish in the first frame.

Simon is a cheerful little planet of six million people and one law, which is the game. Simon has been in charge since before he could talk, which was a very quiet year. Now he talks all the time. The whole planet hops to work, pudding comes first and the cat is Chancellor of the Exchequer. Grown-ups listen very carefully for the first two words.

I've been holding mine for three days. Simon says I'm doing very well.

(In France, the same game is called 'Jacques a dit': Jacques said. Nobody has told Simon about Jacques.)

Would you have woken him?
