Invented planet No. 015: Charlie. Every Charlie gets this planet.

Frame one: find Charlie Cup.

There are thirty-eight people on Charlie, and every one of them is Charlie. Shout 'Charlie!' in the market and the whole street turns round. Babies are Charlie Nothing until they grab something, which is why nobody here wakes a baby. Hands in pockets count as holding nothing, so you can take a day off anywhere.

The morning after the party, thirty-seven Charlies were on holiday, so I dried. For a week I was Charlie Tea Towel.

Welsh villages with too many Joneses did the same thing: Jones the Milk, Jones the Post.
