Imaginary planet No. 015: Charlie. Every Charlie gets this planet.

There are forty-one people on Charlie, and all of them are called Charlie. They tried surnames, but those turned out to be Charlie as well. So now everyone goes by whatever they're holding. Charlie Spade does the gardens. Charlie Spoon runs the café. Charlie Fish is not to be trusted with the café. Charlie Nothing is having a day off.

How many Charlies do you know?
