Invented planet No. 041: Miles. A planet for people who never ask anyone for anything.

One lamp, ninety miles from the next.

Miles is a huge, flat, windy world with 52,000 people on it, most of them a day's drive from anyone else. So the planet runs on lamps: one flash at dusk for 'All fine', two for 'Come'. On a clear night every farm can see five or six others, and everyone knows whose is whose. Edie had lived alone at the top of the valley for years, and had never once flashed twice.

I flashed twice on my first night, by accident, looking for the switch. Nine trucks and a vet.

(On Earth, the story goes, the outback radio network kept a 'galah session', a daily slot when people on remote stations could just chat, named after a very noisy bird. On Miles, they flash.)

When did you last ask anyone for anything?
