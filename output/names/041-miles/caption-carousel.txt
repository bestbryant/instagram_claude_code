Imaginary planet No. 041: Miles. A planet for people who never ask anyone for anything.

On Miles, next door is ninety miles away, so nobody pops round. At dusk, every farm shines a lamp at the sky: once means 'All fine', twice means 'Come'. Edie had flashed once every dusk of her life. She'd never asked anyone for anything. In January, she flashed twice. By dawn, two hundred people had driven through the night. She'd made too much soup.
