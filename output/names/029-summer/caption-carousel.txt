Imaginary planet No. 029: Summer. A planet for every nan who was right all along.

On Summer, it snows once every eighty years. Nan was six the last time, and she has mentioned it at every wedding and every funeral since. 'It's cold,' she says, 'and it squeaks.' At school, snow is taught next to dragons. Then, one morning, it snowed.

Names welcome below. I find a few planets each evening.
