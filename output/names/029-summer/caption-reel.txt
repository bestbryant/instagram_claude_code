A planet for every nan who was right all along. (Imaginary planet No. 029: Summer)

Somewhere in that heatwave is Nan.

Summer: 210,000 people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and one snowfall every eighty years, give or take. The rest of the time it's summer. Coats are museum pieces and 'cold' is something you say about soup. Nan was six the last time. She has described it at every family occasion since, in detail, while everyone else fanned themselves and exchanged looks.

(On Earth, snow only squeaks underfoot when it's colder than about minus ten. So it was a cold one. She'd like that noted.)

What did your nan swear was true?

Tell me a name in the comments. I haven't found one without a planet yet.
