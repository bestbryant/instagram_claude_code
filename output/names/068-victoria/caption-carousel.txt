Imaginary planet No. 068: Victoria. A planet for whoever brings cake to work.

On Victoria, Monday doesn't end until somebody brings in a cake. Nobody knows whether it's a law or the weather, and nobody has wanted to find out. Most offices manage it by the first tea break. At Sue's, it's always Sue: a Victoria sponge, jam and cream, in a tin with a robin on the lid, and Tuesday by lunchtime. Then Sue went to Spain. Brian tried biscuits. Biscuits don't count. It's day nine of Monday, the bins haven't gone out, and nobody has been paid.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
