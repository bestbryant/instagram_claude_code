A new planet twice a day. Any name you leave below gets one.

Invented planet No. 068: Victoria. A planet for whoever brings cake to work.

Sue's the one holding the tin.

Victoria: 4.2 million people, a 25-hour day (Mondays excepted), and a working week that can't get past Monday until somebody brings in a cake. Shop-bought is allowed, but people talk. Most offices manage it by the first tea break. At Sue's, it's always Sue: a Victoria sponge, jam and cream, in a tin with a robin on the lid, and Tuesday by lunchtime. Then Sue went to Spain. Brian tried biscuits. Biscuits don't count.

I brought a cake in, just to be polite. Three offices tried to hire me.

(The story goes that the Victoria sponge is named after Queen Victoria, who liked a slice with her afternoon tea. On Victoria, it's a public service.)

Whose cake would get your office to Tuesday?
