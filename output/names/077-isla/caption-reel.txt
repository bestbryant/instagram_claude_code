Invented planet No. 077: Isla. A planet for anyone not speaking to the in-laws.

Liz has her back to the in-laws, for now.

Isla: 4.6 million people, a 23-hour day, one moon, and a coastline that changes with everyone's moods. A cross word puts a puddle between you. A proper falling-out puts in a sea. Maps are printed in pencil. Liz and her in-laws hadn't spoken since Easter, over a borrowed lawnmower, or possibly a seating plan. Then December came round, and somebody had to sort out Christmas.

I muted a group chat. There's a ferry now.

(On Earth, the sea covers about seven-tenths of the planet, which explains a lot about Christmas.)
