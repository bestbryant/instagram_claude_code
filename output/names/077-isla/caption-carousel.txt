Imaginary planet No. 077: Isla. A planet for anyone not speaking to the in-laws.

On Isla, falling out makes islands. Stop speaking to someone and the sea comes in between you, and stays until somebody rings. Liz and her in-laws fell out at Easter, over something nobody can remember. By autumn you could sail between them. Then Liz rang about Christmas, and the water was gone before she'd hung up. They argued about the sprouts.
