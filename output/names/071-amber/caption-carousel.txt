Imaginary planet No. 071: Amber. A planet for every mum in the passenger seat.

On Amber, the sky works like a traffic light. Red means stay in: storms, mostly, or a new box set. Green means go, and the whole planet goes at once. Amber is rarer, and everybody knows what it means: somebody nearby is learning to drive. Chloe has her test next week. Her mum takes her out after tea, one hand on the dashboard and one foot pressed hard on a brake that isn't there. The sky has been amber since half-term, and her mum has braked all the way home, every time.

Names welcome below. I find a few planets each evening.
