Invented planets, two a day. Leave any name and I'll find its planet.

Invented planet No. 071: Amber. A planet for every mum in the passenger seat.

A nice day for a walk. Not for long.

Amber: 2.9 million people, a 22-hour day, no moons, and a sky that works like a traffic light. Red means stay in: storms, mostly, or a new box set. Green means go, and the whole planet goes at once. Amber is rarer, and everybody knows what it means: somebody nearby is learning to drive. Chloe has her test next week. Her mum takes her out after tea, one hand on the dashboard and one foot pressed hard on a brake that isn't there.

I hired a car to get about. The sky went amber before I'd found the indicator.

(On Earth, the first traffic light went up outside the Houses of Parliament in December 1868. It ran on gas, was worked by a policeman, and exploded within a month. Chloe's mum knows how it felt.)

Who sat in your passenger seat?
