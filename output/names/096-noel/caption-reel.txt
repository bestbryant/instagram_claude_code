Invented planet No. 096: Noel. A planet for every dad up a ladder in December.

Any name you leave gets a planet.

Any first name left below (a person's, a pet's, a nickname) gets its own planet and its own Reel, with thanks to whoever left it.

That glow along the hill is Dad.

Noel: 890,000 people, a 24-hour day, no moons, and Christmas lights bright enough to turn night into day. A string round the porch gets you a decent dawn. A whole street gets you mid-morning. From the first of December the planet doesn't get dark until January, and everybody is very tired and very festive.

I stayed with a family there over Christmas. I asked when it gets dark. They all laughed, then they all yawned.

(The story goes that the first electric Christmas tree lights were put up in 1882 by Edward Johnson, a colleague of Thomas Edison's, in his parlour in New York: eighty bulbs, red, white and blue. The neighbours, presumably, went out and bought eighty-one.)

Is your dad a dawn or a noon?
