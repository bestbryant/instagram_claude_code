Imaginary planet No. 096: Noel. A planet for every dad up a ladder in December.

On Noel, Christmas lights don't twinkle; they turn night into day. A string round the porch gets you a decent dawn. A whole street gets you mid-morning. Dad did the whole hill. Clive next door had a look at it over the fence, went to the shops and came back with noon. Nobody on the street has had a night since the first of December. They nod off in armchairs on the pavement, in broad daylight, in their coats. Dad's bought a bigger ladder.

Any name will do. Leave one and I'll look it up in the atlas.
