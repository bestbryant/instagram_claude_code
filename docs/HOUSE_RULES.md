# Atlas of Elsewhere: house rules for story episodes

Settled by the head writer after the round-3 final pass (19 October 2026), from the 20-person writers' room's proposals. The registry below is the slate as shipped; add a row for every new episode. These apply to every future episode. Each rule credits the proposal it comes from; builds that sharpened a rule are folded into it.

1. **One registry, five-post spacing** (P-standup-1.2, with P-animated-short-1.3, P-virality-2.0, P-serial-mystery-1.1): every merge fills its row in the registry below before it ships. No ending shape, turn palette, turn device, question shape, pointer opener or held-object crowd repeats within five posts. A phrase, sentence template, clock time or named number belongs to the planet whose premise it is (otherwise the earlier post) and is never reused.
2. **Pay off on an identical camera** (P-nature-doc-1.1): the setup and the payoff share a preset or an explicit focus and zoom, with one thing changed. A sign appears only in its own beat unless it is a frame-1 find, the overlay never quotes it, and a sign rewrite does one job (the turn or the button).
3. **Hand-set line breaks, linted** (P-poet-1.0): break every line by hand at a phrase boundary and check it with atlas.text.wrap at W_SAFE in its real font (84 serif for openers, 64 italic for beats). Use at most 3 lines, put the payload alone on the last line, and never end a line on a, the, in, at, of or to.
4. **The loop test and a 6-word end card** (P-flash-fiction-1.0, P-poet-1.2): the opener is 8 words or fewer and the end line 6 or fewer. Read the end line, then the opener: they read as one sentence, or frame 1 means something new. Set the entry's text_y.name whenever a sun, moon or giant sits behind y 0.445.
5. **Runtime budget, real arithmetic, one sheet** (P-short-video-1.1, webcomic-1.4): 60-85 words and 30-37 s counting the kicker and the end line, which apply_episodes.py does not count. Include a line of 5 words or fewer in the first 6 s and a turn line of 5 words or fewer, plus one preview sheet per merge. Entry fields (text_y, hook, kicker, extra, story) go in a top-level entry_changes, because --write drops them.
6. **Real calendar, kept promises** (P-serial-mystery-1.0, P-nature-doc-1.4): only a post with a not_before row may say 'tonight' or name a weekday as the viewer's own, and undated episodes say 'one night' or 'four minutes later'. Every 'in the comments' needs a dated comment row that the routine posts with publish.comment(). Never promise 'pinned', because only the owner can pin.
7. **One true, checkable thing per caption** (P-hard-sf-1.3, P-folklore-1.3): a physics number for a premise planet or an Earth custom (hedged with 'the story goes') for a custom planet. Never both, never on screen, placed after the setting and before the question, and ideally inverted in one closing clause. Check dates and origins against a source.
8. **Caption hygiene and the epilogue** (P-community-2.0, P-community-1.4): the episode caption never contains '#', 'Imaginary planet No.' or a comment ask, because captions.reel adds them (lint the invite phrases, not 'below' or 'comment'). It ends on one in-world question that won't parse as a name request. The epilogue is 20 words or fewer, has no ask and never explains the joke.
9. **Ration board turns** (P-standup-2.0): at most one episode in any three consecutive posts turns on rewriting its signpost, never two on the same day, and only where the board is the premise (Luna, Bella). Kess's swap is a button.
10. **Ages only for arithmetic; alternate herd and individual** (P-nature-doc-1.2): a local gets an age only if the number does a sum on screen. Avoid three single-person episodes in a row wherever fixed dates allow (this slate can't, around Hush and Halloween). A herd episode ends at population scale.
11. **The cartographer's runner** (P-sitcom-1.4, P-romance-1.2): she gets at most one framed beat per episode, never frame 1, the turn or the closer, and not on two posts in a row. Turn her off in any shot that frames one lover or one lone figure. Her line in the caption is the planet's rule catching her out (wrong name, wrong custom).
12. **Plant the ending in frame 1, point at it fresh** (P-picture-book-1.1): frame 1 holds the evidence the last beat pays off, and the caption's first line (45 characters or fewer) points at it in new wording. 'Second time through' belongs to Daisy and 'On the replay' to Elsewhere.
